A purchase charged in another **Currency** on a card keeps the original amount and the rate, and adds the card's foreign charge as a separate fee posting when the card has one.

A currency exchange moves money between two of your accounts in different currencies, one of them in your base currency, and may carry a fee. Balances in other currencies are valued with stored prices when a report needs one currency.

An expense, an income or a transfer on an account in another currency asks for its value in the base currency. The value follows the latest stored price on or before the date until you type another amount, and stays empty when no price is stored. In an advanced entry, each line on such an account takes that value under its amount, and the lines balance by it. A transfer between two accounts in different currencies is a currency exchange.

Record all of them on New transaction. The rate is written as a decimal from the charged currency to the card's currency.
