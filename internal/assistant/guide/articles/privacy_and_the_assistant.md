Privacy mode hides every amount on screen, including the assistant's answers and its proposal cards. Turn it on in Settings, in the account menu or in the command palette. It can also turn on by itself when you leave the tab or after 5 minutes idle, in Settings, Privacy; turn it off with the same switch.

The assistant sends your question and the data its tools read to the language model provider chosen in Settings. On a free tier that uses submitted content for training, the panel says so before the first message. Daily limits per provider and model are declared in Settings; when they are reached, or the provider's quota runs out, the assistant shows when it returns.

Model of the conversation. When the chain of the assistant in Settings holds two or more models, the model menu of the composer lists them. Automatic follows the order of the chain and moves to the next model when one runs out. A chosen model answers every next message of this browser and never hands a message to another model: when its quota runs out, the assistant shows when it returns, and another model can be chosen in the menu. The notice before the first message names the provider of the chosen model.

The assistant never changes data by itself: each change is a proposal you confirm. Conversations stay until you delete them.

Files in the conversation. The paperclip of the assistant's composer, a paste or a drop on the composer attaches up to 5 files to a message: OFX, CSV, PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP or text, at most 10 MB each and 50 MB per conversation. A line under the files says what leaves the instance: of an OFX or CSV statement only a summary goes to the provider, with the CSV lines masked as below; a PDF, an image or a text file goes whole when the model reads files, and otherwise the model sees only its name. A sent PDF, image or text file opens from its chip in the message. Importing a statement or prices from a file, or attaching a file to a transaction, is a proposal that names the file, where it goes and what it holds.

Identify columns with AI, on Import statement, sends the header and up to 12 lines of the CSV file to the language model of the use ingestion.csv_columns. Each digit in a line is replaced by 9. A run of three or more letters is replaced by A for each letter; a run of one or two letters is kept. A header cell with a digit is masked the same way.

Views in an answer. When an answer has a figure, a comparison, a share, a trend, a schedule or a list of records, the assistant shows it as a view: a chart, a list or a table that equori builds from the data the assistant read, so its figures are the book's own. Each view states its conclusion first, then the chart, the period it covers and what it was built from. The buttons at its foot open the screen that holds the records, switch between the chart and a table of the same figures, and copy the view as text. Ask for any chart of what the assistant reads, such as your income by month as columns or spending by category as a pie: equori computes every figure of it from your records. A chart can also show a running total, each row's share of the total, or the change from one period to the next. A view of shares switches between amounts and percentages at its top. Hovering or focusing a bar, a point or a cell shows its value. Under a view, up to 3 follow-up questions send themselves when clicked. Privacy mode hides the amounts of a view as it hides every other amount.

The assistant also suggests changes your request implies, such as a category that does not exist yet or a rule for a payee you categorize often. A suggestion shows the label Suggestion and the reason. A card that uses what another card creates waits until you confirm that card; Confirm all confirms the group in order. Undo on a confirmed card runs, once, the change that reverses it: a recorded transaction is voided and stays in its history, a created budget, goal, rule, alias or card is deleted, a closed account reopens, a hidden security shows again, a linked or skipped occurrence reopens, a reviewed investment movement returns to review, and a categorized or corrected transaction returns to the version it held before, with the earlier version kept in its history. A void, a deletion, a merge, a transfer or reimbursement made from a transaction and an import have no Undo; ask the assistant to correct them.

What the assistant does not do. Every other action a member takes on a screen has a tool. For each line below the assistant says so and names the screen; it does not look for a tool that comes close.

- session: signing in and out, the second factor, passkeys and recovery codes, creating a book, switching to another book, leaving a book and accepting an invitation. They change who is signed in or which book the session reads. The account menu and Settings hold them.
- secret: access tokens, the credentials of a bank integration and a new bank connection. A secret typed in a conversation would be stored with it. Settings and the Links screen hold them.
- credential: inviting a person to the book and the password of the backup copy. Each answers or takes a token or a password. Settings holds them.
- llm: the language model providers, their keys, the chain of models of each use, the daily limits and the list of conversations. The assistant does not change its own model, keys or limits. Settings holds them; a conversation is deleted in the panel.
- erase: deleting the book and restoring an archive over it. Both erase records and ask the owner to type the name of the book. Settings, Data holds them.
- instance: the backup of the instance, the diagnostic file, the version and the update check, the market data sources, the sync schedule and the invitation of a new account. They belong to the administrator of the instance, not to a book. Settings holds them, for the administrator.
- binary: the content of an attachment and of an export archive. The assistant names the file or gives the path that downloads it.
- capture: the reading of typed lines on the Capture screen. The assistant reads the same text in the conversation and proposes the transactions with record_transaction.
