A **Transaction** has a date, a description, an optional **Payee**, optional tags, and two or more **Posting** lines. The **Sign convention**: a positive amount increases an asset or an expense; a negative amount increases a liability, an income or equity. A purchase of 50 on a checking account posts -50 to checking and +50 to the expense category.

A **Split** gives one transaction several categories. A **Correction** creates a new version of the transaction and keeps the earlier one in its history with the reason. Voiding removes a transaction from balances and reports and keeps it in history. A selection on Transactions is voided in one step with one reason; a transaction with reconciled postings is refused and named in the result.

Record transactions on New transaction or Text capture; open one on Transaction detail; change it on Correct transaction. A purchase, a sale or an investment income opens there in its own form, with the stored values and a reason; a later sale of the same investment blocks the change. A transaction waiting for a category shows in Transactions with the uncategorized filter. The search box of Transactions matches every word typed, in any order and ignoring case and accents, against the description, the payee and the posting memos. The Amount filter keeps the transactions that move an exact value, at least a value, at most a value or a range of values in the base currency. Transactions leaves voided transactions out of the list; the Show voided switch beside the filters brings them back, struck through, and the browser keeps the choice. On New transaction, the account field opens a sheet that groups the accounts by what choosing one does with the amount: the accounts the amount leaves or enters, the cards whose statement receives it or that it pays, and the other accounts in a collapsed block. The account most used for the category or the payee comes first, with the count of recent transactions behind it. A figure of the balance after the entry shows only when the entry would overdraw the account or take money out of a reserve; a card shows the due date of the statement and is unavailable when its free limit is below the amount.

The period control in the header of the transactions list and of an account steps to the period before or after with its arrows, and its label opens the presets and a calendar for a run of days. A dot before the label marks a period that holds today, and the button Today returns to the current month.
