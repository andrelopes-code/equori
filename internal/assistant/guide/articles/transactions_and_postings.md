A **Transaction** has a date, a description, an optional **Payee**, optional tags, and two or more **Posting** lines. The **Sign convention**: a positive amount increases an asset or an expense; a negative amount increases a liability, an income or equity. A purchase of 50 on a checking account posts -50 to checking and +50 to the expense category.

A **Split** gives one transaction several categories. A **Correction** creates a new version of the transaction and keeps the earlier one in its history with the reason. Voiding removes a transaction from balances and reports and keeps it in history. A selection on Transactions is voided in one step with one reason; a transaction with reconciled postings is refused and named in the result.

The New transaction screen records one expense, income or transfer at a time: the kind, the amount, the account, the date and the category, then Save. Its account list separates the accounts that pay at once from the credit cards, whose expense enters a statement. Save and add another keeps the kind, the account and the date. The question mark of its header opens the help page Record a transaction (/help/record-transaction), with figures and a video of the steps.

On Transactions, a double click opens a transaction in a panel beside the list; its "Transaction actions" menu holds "Correct" and "Void". "Correct" opens the correction form, which asks for the "Reason for the correction" and keeps the earlier version in the history; "Void" asks for a "Reason" of at most 500 characters. "Show voided" lists voided transactions. The question mark of the correction form opens the help page Correct or void a transaction (/help/correct-transaction).

Record transactions on New transaction or Text capture; open one on Transaction detail; change it on Correct transaction. A purchase, a sale or an investment income opens there in its own form, with the stored values and a reason; a later sale of the same investment blocks the change. A transaction waiting for a category shows in Transactions with the uncategorized filter. The search box of Transactions matches every word typed, in any order and ignoring case and accents, against the description, the payee and the posting memos. The Amount filter keeps the transactions that move an exact value, at least a value, at most a value or a range of values in the base currency. Transactions leaves voided transactions out of the list; the Show voided switch beside the filters brings them back, struck through, and the browser keeps the choice. On New transaction, the account field opens a sheet that groups the accounts by what choosing one does with the amount: the accounts the amount leaves or enters, the cards whose statement receives it or that it pays, and the other accounts in a collapsed block. The account most used for the category or the payee comes first, with the count of recent transactions behind it. A figure of the balance after the entry shows only when the entry would overdraw the account or take money out of a reserve; a card shows the due date of the statement and is unavailable when its free limit is below the amount.

On a phone, Transactions reads as a statement: the transactions grouped by day, each day with what it changed, money in less money out, and each row with its category and accounts under the title and the amount at the end, money in with a plus sign. Tapping a row opens Transaction detail.

The period control in the header of the transactions list and of an account steps to the period before or after with its arrows, and its label opens the presets and a calendar for a run of days. A dot before the label marks a period that holds today, and the button Today returns to the current month.

Transaction detail of an expense holds "Paid by a goal": choosing a goal states that money saved in that goal paid the expense, and "No goal" removes it. The section is not drawn on a phone.

The tools. search_transactions finds transactions and gives their IDs; get_transaction returns one in full: postings, links to refunds and reimbursements, earlier versions with their reasons, attachment names, disputes and the plan of an installment purchase; with include_reimbursement it also lists what can repay or be repaid. On Transaction detail a file is attached to a transaction or removed from it; attach_file does the same with file_id, the id of the [file ...] line of a file the user attached to the conversation, and transaction_id, and detach_attachment removes one with attachment_id, from get_transaction; the stored file is deleted when no other transaction holds it.

record_transaction records a new transaction. It needs date, description, amount (positive, in the currency of from_account_id) and from_account_id, the account or category the money leaves. to_account_id is where the money arrives: a purchase goes from a checking, cash or card account to an expense category, an income from an income category to an asset account, a transfer between two own accounts. splits replaces to_account_id with several destinations whose amounts add up to amount. payee_id and tags are optional. On a credit card, installments (2 to 48) records an installment purchase, and original_amount with original_currency and exchange_rate records a foreign purchase. Between accounts in two currencies, to_amount is the amount received, fee the fee in the base currency and fee_category_id its category.

change_transaction changes one transaction with transaction_id and action. correct changes the fields given (date, description, payee_id, tags, amount, from_account_id, to_account_id) and keeps reason in the history; on an installment purchase it changes only description, payee_id and tags. make_transfer replaces the category side with to_account_id, another own account. reimburse makes this incoming transaction the repayment of recorded expenses, listed in allocations with the amount each takes. A category is set with categorize_transactions, not here.

categorize_transactions gives one category_id, and optionally one payee_id, to transaction_ids, at most 50 per call. It does not split a transaction among categories: change_transaction does.

void_transaction voids a transaction with transaction_id and reason: it stops counting in balances and reports and stays in the history with the reason. A voided transaction is not restored; it is recorded again.
