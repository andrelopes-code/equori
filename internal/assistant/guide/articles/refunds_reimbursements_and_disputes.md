A **Refund** credits all or part of a card purchase, linked to it by a **Link**. A **Chargeback** is the refund that follows a dispute you won; opening a dispute keeps the purchase on the statement until it is resolved.

A **Reimbursement** is money someone pays you back for an expense you recorded, such as a **Shared expense**: the incoming transaction is linked to the expenses it repays and reduces them in your reports.

Record refunds and disputes on Statement or Transaction detail; link a reimbursement on Transaction detail of the incoming transaction.

On an incoming transaction, the "Transaction actions" menu of its panel holds "Mark as reimbursement": the dialog shows at its top the amount received, the amount distributed and what stays as income, then lists the expenses of the previous 90 days with an amount left; a checked expense takes its amount in its own row, and an expense left at zero or an excess is named under the figures. An uncategorized inflow asks "Does this reimburse recent expenses?" with "Match to expenses". On an expense, "Record reimbursement received" lists the payments received of the next 90 days with an amount free. The help page Record a reimbursement (/help/reimbursement) shows the steps with figures and a video.
