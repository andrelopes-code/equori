A Brokerage holds one Investment line per security. Its money is in a Cash account inside it or in an account of the same institution, chosen on the New account screen. Two steps record a purchase: create a brokerage, then record a buy. The first buy of a security creates its Investment line; no line is created by hand. A trade buys or sells for the Cash or for an open account of the institution; the **Position** keeps the quantity and the **Cost basis**; a sale removes cost at **Average cost**, or the oldest purchase first for fixed income in Brazil. get_holding_lots reads this for one Investment line: the method (fifo or average), the quantity, the cost basis and, under fifo, each open purchase with its date, quantity and cost at the end of a date. **Investment income** (dividends) is paid into the Cash or that account and may have tax withheld.

The **Market value** of a position uses the latest **Price** of its **Instrument**. Fixed income terms accrue a daily price from the latest manual or official one. Defining an instrument shows the quote of the enabled sources. probe_quote does the same before manage_instrument define: with code, type, quote_currency and, when needed, exchange and symbols, it returns the latest quote of each enabled source that covers the security, or the error of the source, and the sources that are disabled; it stores nothing and needs the write role. record_price_series records several dated prices of one instrument at once, all or nothing, with code, quote_instrument and prices; a price already typed for a date is replaced, and a date a source covers keeps the price of the source (below_feed). Its page opens with the latest price and its change, a chart of the range with manual prices and the average cost (drag to see a period's change), the position with its unrealized result and a fixed income term with liquidity and grace date; a listed real estate fund with a stored report shows the fund report: the figures the fund filed for its latest month, the price over the book value, the distributions of twelve months over the price and the break-even growth against the longest bond of the real curve, and equori gives no recommendation; when two funds of the source report the same code, the page lists both with their segment and identifier and Link stores the chosen one as the symbol of the source (get_market_data view fund_reports returns them as fund_report_candidates; manage_instrument links one by symbols); a stock whose company files with the regulator shows the company statements: return on equity and net margin over twelve months, net debt over equity, the years with profit among the five latest, and the nine Piotroski signals as facts without a sum; equori gives no recommendation; the prices by date and the sources with dates open in layers; a table of yearly price changes; it ends with links to other sites. It loads and imports quotes, sets its symbol at a source, retypes it, hides it and removes it when unused. The list shows the price date and the changes over a week, month, quarter, year and 12 months.

The Portfolio screen adds positions and Cash into one value, with the gain over cost, the income of the year, the split by type, currency or institution and a 3-month price line per position. Cash goes below zero when a purchase was recorded without the transfer of the money to the brokerage: its row in the split shows a dash, the other shares add up to 100%, and recording the transfer corrects it. A notice names holdings with a late or no quote. The Reports screen holds every asset account and **Net worth**. get_portfolio view net_worth reads assets, liabilities and their difference on a date; view net_worth_days reads the same at the end of each day from from to to, at most 62 days.

During a trading session the portfolio values a holding at the current quote its source reports and shows the hour of that quote beside the value. While the session is open the value is marked as partial: it changes until the close. The history, the days before today and every recorded transaction use the close of each day, never a partial value. A holding whose source reports no current quote, such as fixed income, a fund or Tesouro Direto, keeps its last close.

The return section of the Portfolio screen measures All brokerages (savings, reserves and checking stay out) or one Brokerage, security, savings account or reserve, over up to 3,660 days. It leads with the gain of the period in money beside the period return, which flows do not change, and its annual rate above a year. Comparisons follow, each over its own window: the daily reference rate on published days, the monthly inflation index over whole published months, with the real return; in Brazil the CDI and the IPCA. Without them, or in another currency, only the period return shows. The block Where the closing balance came from shows opening balance, net deposits (money in minus out), gain (closing minus opening minus net deposits) and closing balance; a bar splits the closing balance into these parts when the gain is positive and no part is negative. The foot writes the annual rate; Details and method holds the reconciliation, rates, credits, the time-weighted return and monthly flows. Yield is categorized under Yield or a sub-category; other income, expense or transfers are money in or out. Savings, reserves and fixed income in the report's currency show their percent of the reference rate; stocks, funds, crypto or foreign currency show the return of the days compared. On the Accounts screen such groups add a 90-day Yield column, and the account page a Yield tab with the same blocks.

Comparing indexers. The calculator "Compare indexers" lists two to six fixed income securities held to one maturity and shows, for each, the gross yield, the tax, the net yield and the net annual rate. The rate of a security tied to an index follows the expected path of that index to the maturity, taken from a survey of expectations. A fixed rate is typed as it is. Beside the break-even of each security tied to an index, against the reference security (the first fixed rate, or else the first security), three figures appear: the expected rate of the index with the date of its survey, the rate of the government bond for the term with the date of its curve, and the inflation implied by government bonds when the index is inflation. A survey older than 11 days and a curve older than 6 days are marked as outdated. The break-even is the constant rate of the index at which the two securities pay the same after tax. The rows keep the order typed. The screen gives no recommendation and no verdict: it computes, and the choice is the user's. It does not compare the issuer's credit risk or the liquidity of each security, and a sale before the maturity has another result. The calculator is listed only when an index of the base currency has an expected path.

The tools. get_portfolio reads the holdings of the book, by view: positions, allocation (by type, currency or institution), net_worth, net_worth_days, return, income_types, subtypes, contribution, fixed_income_events and issuer_exposure. get_market_data reads what the instance knows of the market, by view: instruments, instrument (one, with its fixed income terms), prices, catalog (the securities the enabled sources can quote, searched by code or name), calendars, holidays, indexes, index_rates, index_expectations, curves, fund_reports (the monthly reports of a listed fund, by code) and company_reports (the accounts a listed company filed, by code).

record_investment records a trade or an income; kind is buy, sell or income. The holding is account_id, an Investment line, or brokerage_id with instrument; the first buy of a security at a Brokerage uses the pair and creates the line. A buy or a sell takes date, quantity and unit_price, and optionally fees and taxes. An income takes date, gross, income_type (from get_portfolio view income_types) and category_id, and optionally withheld, the tax withheld at source. cash_account_id is needed only when the Brokerage has more than one paying account. With transaction_id and reason it corrects that trade or income: the call replaces the whole order, so every field that stays is repeated.

manage_instrument acts on a security; action names the operation and code the security.

- define: name, type (currency, stock, etf, reit, depositary_receipt, fixed_income, fund or crypto), quote_currency and scale, the decimal places of a quantity; exchange is the market code (ISO 10383, such as BVMF), subtype refines the type and symbols gives the symbol at a source when it differs from the code.
- rename: name. edit: type (among stock, etf, reit and depositary_receipt), exchange, subtype, symbols, and the issuer with issuer, issuer_scheme and issuer_id.
- record_price: date and price, one price typed by the user.
- set_terms: the terms of a fixed income security (topic fixed_income).
- request_history: from, the first date; the enabled sources load the prices since then.
- hide removes the security from the lists of this book and show brings it back; delete removes a security no book uses.

Instruments and prices are shared by every book of the instance; a contract of record_fixed_income belongs to its book alone.
