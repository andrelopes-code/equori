A Brokerage holds one Investment line per security. Its money is in a Cash account inside it or in an account of the same institution, chosen on the New account screen. Two steps record a purchase: create a brokerage, then record a buy. The first buy of a security creates its Investment line; no line is created by hand. A trade buys or sells for the Cash or for an open account of the institution; the **Position** keeps the quantity and the **Cost basis**; a sale removes cost at **Average cost**, or the oldest purchase first for fixed income in Brazil. **Investment income** (dividends) is paid into the Cash or that account and may have tax withheld.

The **Market value** of a position uses the latest **Price** of its **Instrument**. Fixed income terms accrue a daily price from the latest manual or official one. Defining an instrument shows the quote of the enabled sources. Its page opens with the latest price and its change, a chart of the range with manual prices and the average cost (drag to see a period's change), the position with its unrealized result and a fixed income term with liquidity and grace date; the prices by date and the sources with dates open in layers; a table of yearly price changes; it ends with links to other sites. It loads and imports quotes, sets its symbol at a source, retypes it, hides it and removes it when unused. The list shows the price date and the changes over a week, month, quarter, year and 12 months.

The Portfolio screen adds positions and Cash into one value, with the gain over cost, the income of the year, the split by type, currency or institution and a 3-month price line per position. A notice names holdings with a late or no quote. The Reports screen holds every asset account and **Net worth**.

The return section of the Portfolio screen measures All brokerages (savings, reserves and checking stay out) or one Brokerage, security, savings account or reserve, over up to 3,660 days. It leads with the period return, which flows do not change, and its annual rate above a year. Comparisons follow, each over its own window: the daily reference rate on published days, the monthly inflation index over whole published months, with the real return; in Brazil the CDI and the IPCA. Without them, or in another currency, only the period return shows. The In money panel shows opening balance, net deposits (money in minus out), gain (closing minus opening minus net deposits) and closing balance; a bar splits the growth. Details and method holds the reconciliation, rates, credits, the time-weighted return and monthly flows. Yield is categorized under Yield or a sub-category; other income, expense or transfers are money in or out. Savings, reserves and fixed income in the report's currency show their percent of the reference rate; stocks, funds, crypto or foreign currency show the return of the days compared. On the Accounts screen such groups add a 90-day Yield column, and the account page a Yield tab with the same blocks.
