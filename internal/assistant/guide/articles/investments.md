A Brokerage holds one Investment line per security. Its money is in a Cash account inside it or in an account of the same institution, chosen on the New account screen. Two steps record a purchase: create a brokerage, then record a buy. The first buy of a security creates its Investment line; no line is created by hand. A trade buys or sells for the Cash or for an open account of the institution, as when the checking account pays; the **Position** keeps the quantity and the **Cost basis** at **Average cost**. **Investment income** (dividends) is paid into the Cash or that account and may have tax withheld.

The **Market value** of a position uses the latest **Price** of its **Instrument**. A fixed income security can carry terms that accrue a daily price. Defining an instrument shows the quote of the enabled sources. Its page opens with the latest price and its change, a chart of the range with manual prices and the average cost, the position with its unrealized result and a fixed income term; the prices by date and the sources with dates open in layers; a table lists the price change per year. It loads and imports quotes, sets its symbol at a source, retypes it and removes it when no record uses it. The list shows the price date and the changes over a week, a month, a quarter, the year and 12 months.

The Portfolio screen adds positions and Cash into one value, with the gain over cost, the income of the year, the split by type, currency or institution and a 3-month price line per position. A notice names holdings with a late or no quote. The Reports screen holds every asset account and **Net worth**.

The return section of the Portfolio screen measures a Brokerage, a security, a savings account or a reserve over at most 366 days. Two comparisons follow, each a pair of bars with a chip: against the daily reference rate and the monthly inflation index, the real return. In Brazil: the CDI and the IPCA. A region without them, or a report in another currency than the index, shows only the period return. The In money panel shows the opening balance, the net deposits, the gain and the closing balance; on the Portfolio screen a bar splits the growth into deposits and gain. The Details and method block opens in a layer with the reconciliation of balances, rates, yield credits, the time-weighted return and flows by month. The yield must be categorized under Yield or a sub-category, not a transfer; any other income, expense or transfer is money in or out. A savings account, a reserve or fixed income in the report's currency pays a rate and shows its percent of the reference rate. An account with stocks, funds, crypto or a foreign currency has a market price and shows the return of the days compared, not annualized. On the Accounts screen, such groups add a Yield column over 90 days, with the days compared. The page of such an account has a Yield tab beside Movements, whose last-12-months section draws the same sentence, comparisons, panel and details.
