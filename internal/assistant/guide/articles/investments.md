A Brokerage holds one Cash account and one Investment line per security. Two steps record a purchase: create a brokerage with its Cash on the New account screen, then record a buy. The first buy of a security creates its Investment line; no line is created by hand. A trade buys or sells for the Cash of the Brokerage or for an open account of its institution, as when the checking account pays; the **Position** keeps the quantity and the **Cost basis** at **Average cost**. **Investment income** (dividends) is paid into the Cash or that account and may have tax withheld.

The **Market value** of a position uses the latest stored **Price** of its **Instrument**. A fixed income security can carry terms that accrue a daily price. Defining an instrument searches the enabled sources and shows their quote. Its page opens with the latest price and its change and a chart of the range with manual prices and the average cost marked, then the position with its unrealized result and a fixed income term. The prices by date and the sources with their dates open in layers. It loads and imports quotes, sets its symbol at a source and removes it when no record uses it.

The Portfolio screen adds the positions and the Cash into one value, with the gain over cost, the income of the year and the split by type, currency or institution. A notice names holdings with a late or no quote. The Reports screen holds every asset account and **Net worth**.

The return section of the Portfolio screen measures a Brokerage, a security, a savings account or a reserve over at most 366 days. One sentence states the result first. Two comparisons follow, each a pair of bars on one axis with a chip: against the daily reference rate and the monthly inflation index (the real return). In Brazil these are the CDI and the IPCA. A region without them, or a report in another currency than the index, shows only the period return. The In money panel shows the opening balance, the net deposits (when there were flows), the gain and the closing balance; on the Portfolio screen a bar splits the growth into deposits and gain. The Details and method block opens in a layer and holds the reconciliation of the balances, the rates, the yield credits, the time-weighted return and the flows by month. The yield must be categorized under Yield or a sub-category, not a transfer; any other income, expense or transfer is money in or out. A savings account, a reserve or fixed income in the report's currency pays a rate and shows its percent of the reference rate. An account with stocks, funds, crypto or a foreign currency has a market price and shows the return of the days compared, not annualized. A savings account with no earlier yield is compared from its first. On the Accounts screen, such groups add a Yield column over 90 days, with the days compared. The page of such an account has a Yield tab beside Movements, whose last-12-months section draws the same sentence, comparisons, panel and details.
