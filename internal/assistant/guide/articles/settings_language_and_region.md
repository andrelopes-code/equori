Your language sets the language of the app and of the assistant's answers. It follows you on every device, as your appearance choices do. In Appearance, Text, amount colour shows money in in green and a loss in red, or only the sign. Summary figures write the amounts of summary cards in full, without decimals or in compact form; tables keep every digit. With more than one book, Book colour gives each book its own accent.

The region of your finances sets how numbers and dates are written and which regional rules apply, such as the business-day calendar and tax rules of investments. The time zone sets which day is today and when each month starts; until one is chosen, the region's zone applies.

The base currency is chosen when your finances are created and can change only before the first transaction. Language model providers, the chain of models of each use and daily limits are in the Assistant section of Settings.

Settings, Market data, lists the price sources an admin enables. Each source states how many instruments it quotes and how many history requests are pending. A source marked Not official asks for a confirmation before it is enabled; its quotes fill only the dates that no official source covers.

A source marked "Current quotes" also reports the value of an instrument during the session. equori asks it only while a member reads a valuation, at most once a minute per instrument. When the request fails, the row states the failure and the screens use the last close.

Change the language in Settings, Language, and the region, time zone and base currency in Settings, Finances, or ask the assistant to change language or region. An owner can also ask the assistant to rename the book, change its region or time zone, change the role of a member, remove a member or revoke an invitation; each change waits for the owner's confirmation. The assistant does not invite members and does not delete the book.

Settings, Data, exports every record as an archive and restores an archive into finances without transactions. For finances that already hold transactions, Replace content restores the archive after the owner types the name of the book: it deletes every record, the assistant conversations and the assistant usage, and keeps the book, its members and its access tokens. A refused import keeps the previous records. Export with credentials also seals the credentials of the integrations and the keys of the language model providers under a passphrase of 8 to 256 characters that equori does not store. The dialog proposes a generated passphrase of 25 characters; save it before confirming, because it cannot be recovered and uppercase and lowercase letters differ. The owner may replace it with one of their own, typed twice. When importing such an archive, type the same passphrase, or choose to import without the credentials and enter them again in Integrations and in the assistant settings. The providers, the model chains and the daily limits travel with every archive; only the keys need the passphrase. Only an owner exports or imports credentials.
