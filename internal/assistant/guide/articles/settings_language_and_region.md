Your language sets the language of the app and of the assistant's answers. It follows you on every device, as your appearance choices do. In Appearance, Text, amount colour shows money in in green and a loss in red, or only the sign. Summary figures write the amounts of summary cards in full, without decimals or in compact form; tables keep every digit. With more than one book, Book colour gives each book its own accent.

The region of your finances sets how numbers and dates are written and which regional rules apply, such as the business-day calendar and tax rules of investments. The time zone sets which day is today and when each month starts; until one is chosen, the region's zone applies.

The base currency is chosen when your finances are created and can change only before the first transaction. The tool set_base_currency changes it for the owner with base_currency, an ISO 4217 code such as BRL or USD; after the first transaction it is refused (settings_locked), and no tool or screen changes it then. Language model providers, the chain of models of each use and daily limits are in the Assistant section of Settings.

Settings, Market data, lists the price sources an admin enables. Each source states how many instruments it quotes and how many history requests are pending. A source marked Not official asks for a confirmation before it is enabled; its quotes fill only the dates that no official source covers. The market indexes (Ibovespa, IFIX, S&P 500 with dividends, and FTSE Global All Cap through the ETF VT) are sources of this list. An index with a date that should hold a value and does not reads Incomplete with the number of missing values; its history is read again every week. Once a day equori looks for market days without a quote of an instrument and asks every enabled source for them; the page of the instrument states how many no source holds.

A source marked "Current quotes" also reports the value of an instrument during the session. equori asks it only while a member reads a valuation, at most once a minute per instrument. When the request fails, the row states the failure and the screens use the last close.

Change the language in Settings, Language, and the region, time zone and base currency in Settings, Finances, or ask the assistant to change language or region. An owner can also ask the assistant to rename the book, change its region or time zone, change the role of a member, remove a member or revoke an invitation; each change waits for the owner's confirmation. The assistant does not invite members and does not delete the book.

Settings, Data, exports every record as an archive and restores an archive into finances without transactions. request_export starts an export and get_export reads its state (pending, ready or failed) and, when ready, download_path, the path the user opens to download the archive; a second request while one is pending is refused (export_pending). An export requested by the assistant carries no credential of a bank integration: those enter only with a passphrase the owner types on the screen. Restoring an archive is done on the screen only, because it can replace every record of the book. For finances that already hold transactions, Replace content restores the archive after the owner types the name of the book: it deletes every record, the assistant conversations and the assistant usage, and keeps the book, its members and its access tokens. A refused import keeps the previous records. Export with credentials also seals the credentials of the integrations and the keys of the language model providers under a passphrase of 8 to 256 characters that equori does not store. The dialog proposes a generated passphrase of 25 characters; save it before confirming, because it cannot be recovered and uppercase and lowercase letters differ. The owner may replace it with one of their own, typed twice. When importing such an archive, type the same passphrase, or choose to import without the credentials and enter them again in Integrations and in the assistant settings. The providers, the model chains and the daily limits travel with every archive; only the keys need the passphrase. Only an owner exports or imports credentials.

The tools. update_profile changes the language of the member with language, an ISO 639 code such as en or pt; it sets the language of the app and of the assistant's answers. get_book_access reads, for the owner, the name, the region and the time zone of the book, its members with their roles and the pending invitations. manage_book changes what only the owner changes; action names the operation.

- update_book: name (1 to 80 characters), region (an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code such as BR) and time_zone (an IANA name, or default for the zone of the region). The region sets the calendar, the tax rules and the indexes of the book.
- change_member_role: member_id and role: read sees the book, write also records and changes, owner also manages the book and its members.
- remove_member: member_id; the sessions and access tokens of the member end.
- delete_invitation: invitation_id revokes a pending invitation.

Inviting a person and deleting the book are done by the owner on the Settings screen: an invitation carries a token and a deletion erases every record, and neither goes through a conversation.

The tool set_appearance changes the same choices of Appearance, only the ones given: scheme (dark, light, high-contrast or system), density (comfortable, compact or dense), text_size (85, 90, 95, 100, 105, 110, 115, 120, 125, 130, 135 or 140, a percentage of the browser's own), motion (system or reduced), corners (square, soft, standard or round), typeface (geist, system, grotesque, humanist, rounded or mono), amount_tone (colour or sign), summary_amounts (full, whole or compact), visual (standard) and accent (indigo, violet, pink, rose, orange, emerald, cyan, blue, or a colour as #rrggbb). A face installed on the device, the saved themes, the status colours, the surfaces and the colour of each book are chosen on the Appearance screen. The open browser shows a confirmed change at once. get_appearance returns the stored choices; a choice never made is absent.
