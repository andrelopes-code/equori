Bank and private fixed income of a book of region BR for an individual, checked on 2026-11-16. The law changes: the notice of the broker prevails, and a doubt goes to the issuer. Tesouro Direto: get_help topic government_bonds_br.

Subtypes of fixed_income (get_portfolio view subtypes). Taxed at source: cdb, rdb, dpge, lc (letter of exchange), lf (financial letter), lf_subordinated (subordinated financial letter, the last to be paid if the issuer fails), debenture (common), nota_comercial (commercial note, Lei 14.195/2021), lam (letra de arrendamento mercantil, Lei 11.882/2008), coe (certificado de operações estruturadas; its value is the one the issuer reports), tesouro. Exempt interest (Lei 11.033/2004, Lei 12.431/2011, Lei 13.097/2015, Lei 14.937/2024): lci, lca, lh, lcd, cdca, cri, cra, debenture_incentivada, lig, cpr (cédula de produto rural with financial settlement, Lei 11.033/2004 art. 3, V; a CPR settled in goods is not an investment of this kind). lig (letra imobiliária garantida) is exempt for an individual (Lei 13.097/2015) and outside the FGC: its collateral guarantees it. The FGC guarantees up to R$ 250,000.00 per holder per institution or conglomerate and R$ 1,000,000.00 in 4 years (Resolução CMN 4.222/2013, annex II, art. 2) for cdb, rdb, lc, lh, lci, lca and lcd; A dpge (depósito a prazo com garantia especial) has instead the special guarantee of the FGC, up to R$ 40,000,000.00 per holder per conglomerate, apart from the R$ 250,000.00 and outside the 4-year ceiling (FGC material for brokers, December 2025); it cannot be redeemed before maturity and is taxed as a CDB. An lf or an lf_subordinated cannot be redeemed before maturity either (Lei 12.249/2010, Resolução CMN 5.007/2022); a new lf, lf_subordinated or dpge without redeemable_from takes its maturity, and both lf kinds stay outside the FGC. get_portfolio view issuer_exposure shows each issuer against its limits, with special_value for the dpge part. Proposals to tax the exempt kinds were not law on that date.

Income tax of a taxed security, on the gain, by days since each purchase: 22.5% up to 180, 20% from 181 to 360, 17.5% from 361 to 720, 15% above (Lei 11.033/2004, art. 1). A coupon follows the same table. IOF applies to a redemption within 30 days: 96% of the gain on day 1, falling to 3% on day 29, 0 from day 30; the income tax base is the gain less the IOF (Decreto 6.306/2007). A sale removes the cost of the oldest purchase first. estimate_redemption covers the taxed subtypes.

Terms. Indexes: cdi and selic (business days, bus252), ipca and igpm (monthly, act365), and usd_brl, the dollar for a security indexed to the exchange rate (business days: the price of a day is the PTAX selling rate of the business day before over that of the business day before the issue, the D-1 the market uses; state day_count act365, as the stored terms accept bus252 only for cdi or with calendar_id); the cdi, the selic, the igpm and usd_brl come from their BCB SGS series (12, 11, 28655 with the IGP-M to 10 decimals, and 1), and the ipca from the index numbers of the IBGE (SIDRA table 1737), each month the exact ratio of two numbers, once the administrator enables its feed (br_cdi, br_selic, br_ipca, br_igpm, br_ptax_usd); the ipca, the igpm and the selic are kept from July 2000, the base of the government bonds. A pre-fixed rate and the spread over IPCA count the business days of calendar br_banking: day_count bus252 with calendar_id br_banking. The statement of the issuer is recorded with record_price.

Minimum term of an LCI or an LCA. A new LCI or LCA recorded without redeemable_from takes the first day the rule of its issue date allows a redemption: 6 months for one without a price index issued from 2025-08-01 (Resolução CMN 5.215/2025); 12 months (LCI) or 9 months (LCA) for one issued from 2024-02-02 to 2025-07-31 (Resolução CMN 5.119/2024); 36 months for an LCI updated by IPCA or IGP-M issued from 2024-02-02; 90 days before 2024-02-02. An LCA updated by a price index takes no default: the user states its date. A date the user gives prevails.

Progressive rate (CDB progressivo). A rate that rises with the time held is written with set_rate_steps after the terms: each step names after_days, the days held from the issue date, and the percent of the index and the spread from then; the terms' own rate holds before the first step. apply is retroactive when the issuer pays the rate reached for the whole period since the issue, and stepped when each rate pays from its own day; the statement of the issuer says which. set_terms replaces the whole terms, so the steps are written again after it.

Indexer comparison. The sources are the CDI and the IPCA of the instance, the Focus survey of the Banco Central do Brasil for the expected rates, and the curves of Tesouro Direto for the market rate of the term and the inflation implied by government bonds. The tax follows the table of the income tax above, and an exempt security pays none. The screen gives no recommendation and no verdict.
