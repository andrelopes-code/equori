The Contribution plan screen answers where a contribution goes to bring the portfolio to a target split. Open it from Portfolio with the Contribution plan link.

A plan holds classes, such as fixed income or domestic stocks, each with a target share; the targets add up to 100%. A class holds assets (investment accounts and instruments) and may split its share among them with targets of their own. The table shows each class with its target, its current share, its share after the contribution and the amount to contribute.

The split only buys. Each class receives the amount that raises its share toward the target, a class above its target receives nothing, and no **Position** is sold. The Shopping list shows the purchases, with an approximate quantity for an asset that has a **Price**.

Edit targets opens the table for change. A target moves by typing a percent or by the slider, and the other classes absorb the difference in proportion to their targets. A lock keeps a class or an asset at its target while the others move. Save stores the plan; Discard drops the change.

A position that no class holds is listed under Outside the plan, with a selector to put it in a class.

Without a plan, the screen offers Start from my portfolio, which makes one class per instrument type held with each target equal to its current share, and Start from scratch, which opens one class at 100% to name.

An asset with no price or no exchange rate today makes the plan incomplete. The screen lists each such asset as a link to its instrument page and keeps the targets. No current share, after share or amount shows until the price or rate is recorded.
