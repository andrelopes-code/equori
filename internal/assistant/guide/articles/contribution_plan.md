The Contribution plan screen answers where a contribution goes to bring the portfolio to the split the user wants. Open it from Portfolio with the Contribution plan link.

A plan holds groups, such as fixed income or domestic stocks, each with a target share. The targets add up to 100%. A group holds assets (investment accounts and instruments) and may split its share among them with targets of their own.

The tools. get_portfolio view contribution reads the plan valued today; with amount it also shows where that contribution goes and stores nothing. save_contribution_plan writes the plan and replaces all of it: read the current plan first and repeat every group that stays; an empty classes list removes the plan. A group is a class: name (1 to 60 characters, unique: contribution_class_name_duplicate), target_bp (basis points: 4000 is 40%) and members. The classes add up to 10000 (contribution_target_sum), at most 20. A member is account_id, an account that counts whole such as a fixed income Investment line or a reserve, or instrument, a code counted in every Brokerage that holds it; never both, and a holding belongs to one class only (contribution_member_duplicate, contribution_member_invalid). Member targets are optional: either no member of a class has target_bp or they add up to 10000 (contribution_member_target_sum). amount is the monthly contribution in the base currency, positive (contribution_amount_invalid); absent stores none. A group may hold questions (at most 10, each 1 to 200 characters, unique in the group: contribution_question_invalid): yes-or-no questions the investor writes. Each member of such a group carries answers, one boolean per question in order, and no target_bp (contribution_answers_invalid); its target_bp is derived: its share of the answers true of the group, in basis points summing to 10000, the remainder to the largest fractions. With every answer false the group has no member targets and question_ideals is false. equori writes no question.

A group may instead hold rules (at most 5, in rules, with empty members; a group has members or rules, never both). A rule is a set of conditions that a security must all meet: instrument_type, subtypes, quote_currency, return_kind (rate, inflation or fixed), matures_from_year and matures_to_year (the first and last maturity year), daily_liquidity and guarantee. A security that meets any rule of the group joins it, including a security bought later. When several groups claim one security, the group with an instrument member that names it wins, otherwise the first group in plan order with a rule it meets, otherwise the security is listed outside the plan. guarantee takes covered (under the deposit guarantee of the base currency, named in guarantee_name), sovereign (government bond) or uncovered. get_portfolio view contribution returns rules and matched (each security taken by a rule, with value and share_bp inside the group); an entry of outside carries its return_kind. The return kind, maturity and daily liquidity of a contract come from its terms: fixed without an index, inflation with a monthly index, rate with a daily one. A government bond without terms is read from its code: LFT is rate with daily liquidity, LTN and NTN-F are fixed, NTN-B, NTN-B Principal, NTN-C, RendA+ and Educa+ are inflation, each maturing on the date of its code and counting as sovereign. daily_liquidity true means a redemption on any business day near the accrued value, so only an LFT among government bonds meets it. uncovered is the private credit of the book (a debenture, a CRI, a CRA, an LF) and never takes a government bond or a stock; a stock or a fund never meets a condition on return kind, maturity or liquidity. A security taken by a rule without a price or a rate today makes the plan incomplete, as a member does. A group by rule suits fixed income bought each month: a new contract joins its group with no edit of the plan, and the plan names no security before the purchase.

The screen has three parts. This month holds the contribution amount, the portfolio today and after this contribution, one sentence with the groups and assets that receive money, and the Shopping list with an approximate quantity for an asset that has a **Price**. Portfolio against the target draws one bar per group with its share today, its share after this contribution and its target, and says in words whether the group is below, above or at the target. A group that receives no money says so.

The split only buys. Each group receives the amount that raises its share toward the target, a group above its target receives nothing, and no **Position** is sold. Without an amount, the screen asks for one and shows no purchase.

Edit groups and targets turns the rows into editors. A target moves by typing a percent or by the slider, and the other groups absorb the difference in proportion to their targets. A lock keeps a group or an asset at its target while the others move. The Assets (N) button of a group row expands the assets of the group with Add asset and the switch Split the group's target among its assets, which gives each asset a target inside the group. In the assets panel of a group whose target is divided, Target of each asset chooses Typed or By my questions. By my questions lists the investor's questions (at most 10, none written by equori), one checkbox per question on each asset, the count of yes, the derived target of the asset, and a line of facts: the price over book value and the distribution of twelve months of a fund report, or the return on equity, margin, net debt over equity and years with profit of a company report. The Remove group button at the end of the row deletes the group. Save stores the plan. Discard drops the change. A group without a name blocks the split and the save.

In the assets panel of a group, the choice "Chosen assets" | "By rule" sets how the group gathers its assets. Switching a group that holds chosen assets to "By rule" asks for confirmation, then drops the chosen assets; switching back drops the rules the same way. With "By rule", "Add rule" opens a dialog with the conditions of one rule: Kind of investment, Subtypes, Form of return, Maturity (From and To years), Daily liquidity, Guarantee (shown when the region of the book has one) and Quote currency. "Save rule" stays disabled while every choice is "Any". Each rule shows in one line with the count and value of the securities that meet it today; the edit and remove buttons change or delete it. A group has at most 5 rules. Every held security that meets one of the rules joins the group, including the ones bought later. An asset chosen in a group counts before any rule, and among rules the security joins the first group of the list. The guarantee words are "covered by" the guarantee fund of the region, "government bond" and "no guarantee"; a book of a region without a guarantee fund shows "no guarantee in the book's region" and the rule meets nothing. A group by rule receives its amount as a value, with no split among its securities.

A position that no group holds is listed under Outside the plan, with a selector to put it in a group.

Without a plan, the screen explains how it works and offers Start from my portfolio and Start from scratch, which opens one group at 100% to name. Start from my portfolio makes one group by rule per return kind of the fixed income held (Floating, Inflation-linked, Fixed rate, each with the rule instrument_type fixed_income and its return_kind), and one group per other instrument type held with its positions as chosen assets; a fixed income contract without terms joins the Fixed income group as a chosen asset. Each target equals the group's share today.

An asset with no price or no exchange rate today makes the plan incomplete. The sentence in This month names each such asset as a link to its instrument page, and the screen keeps the targets. No amount to contribute shows until the price or rate is recorded.

A row of the Shopping list bought by value, with no quantity, has the button "compare indexers" in its order cell when an index of the base currency has an expected path. It opens a dialog that holds the indexer comparison with the cost of the row as the amount. The comparison shows the yield after tax of two to six fixed income securities held to one maturity and gives no recommendation. Closing the dialog changes nothing in the plan, the split or the Shopping list. A row of a listed fund with a stored report has the button "see report", which opens the fund report of that fund and changes nothing in the plan.

Under the contribution amount, when the month plan of the Budgets tab already sends money to a brokerage account through savings or transfer schedules, one line states the scheduled savings and the goal deposits it sends, and "Use {amount}" fills the field with their sum; the amount is stored only on save. An asset whose account a goal holds carries the tag "reserved for {goal}": money bought there is saved for that goal.

The question mark of the Contribution plan header opens the help page The contribution plan (/help/contribution-plan), with figures of This month, the Shopping list, Portfolio against the target and Edit groups and targets.
