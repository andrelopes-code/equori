A **Budget** sets a monthly amount for an expense category from a month on. Each month shows the planned amount, what was carried from the previous month when rollover is on, what was spent in the category and its subcategories, and what remains available.

Budgets use the **Accrual view**: a card purchase counts in the month of its date, not when the statement is paid.

The Track view of the budgets tab shows the month over every budget in one line of four figures (spent, left, committed, overspent) and one table line per budget under its top category; the spending no budget covers and where the spending went stay closed at the end until opened; a budget's page shows its amounts by month and compares planned and spent. A budget created by mistake is deleted from the actions of its line or from its page; its category and transactions stay. The assistant also creates and deletes budgets, changes their amounts and reports their progress.

The **Income split** divides the amount to distribute into buckets, such as 50/30/20 or six buckets for fixed costs, comfort, goals, pleasures, financial freedom and knowledge. Each bucket has a name, a share and how it counts: spend up to the target, use at least the target, take what goes into savings (transfers into the savings, reserve and brokerage accounts that are not goal accounts, one bucket) or deposit into goals (what moved into the goals' accounts from other accounts, one bucket); the shares sum to 100%. A transfer counts in one bucket: a deposit into a goal account counts in the goals bucket, a deposit into another savings account in the savings bucket, and a withdrawal counts against its month. Income not spent and not transferred counts in no bucket. Each expense category goes in one bucket, a subcategory follows its category unless it gets its own. The Plan view lists each bucket with its target and what the budgets plan for it, and groups the plan by bucket. The Track view says how many buckets missed their target in the month and shows, per bucket, what is left or over against its share of the income received and whether it kept to the target in each of the last six months; the bucket that takes savings holds what the month left. Set up split or Edit split opens the dialog, which starts from a preset or from scratch; the Categories screen opens it too, shows the bucket of each category and sets it in the category form. The assistant also reads and saves the split.
