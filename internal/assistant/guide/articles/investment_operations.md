A buy or sell is recorded on the New transaction screen as a trade of a Brokerage. An order filled at several prices takes "Order total" in place of the unit price: equori divides the total by the quantity, to 12 decimals, so the gross equals the total to the cent.

The "Settlement" date is optional. Empty, it takes the settlement cycle of the instrument's exchange: two business days on the B3 since 2019-05-27, three before. The position, the cost and the result are dated on the trade; the cash of the institution's view moves on the settlement date, which is the date a bank import matches.

A code of the B3 odd-lot market (the round-lot code with a final F, such as PIBB11F) is the same security: a trade typed with it is recorded on the round-lot investment, and defining an instrument with it is refused with the code to define instead.

In a Brazilian book, a sale first closes the purchases of its date at the same brokerage, in order, then the earlier stock. The units that close a purchase of the same date are a day trade: the transaction page lists them with their cost, and the realized results mark the sale with a "Day trade" tag.

A brokerage note records the several orders of one day in one entry. On the new transaction page, the kind "Brokerage note" takes the brokerage, the cash account, the date, one block per order and the costs of the whole note. The costs are split among the orders in proportion to their gross value, and the cents left by the division go one each to the orders with the largest fractions. The withheld income tax is split the same way among the sales. Each order is recorded as a trade of its own. The tool record_brokerage_note does the same: brokerage_id, date (the trade date), settlement_date when the note states it, fees (every cost of the note added up: settlement, emoluments, brokerage, service tax), taxes (the income tax withheld on the note) and orders, 1 to 50, each with side (buy or sell), instrument, quantity and unit_price without costs. cash_account_id is needed only when the brokerage has more than one paying account. A security of the note that is not defined yet is defined first with manage_instrument. The answer lists one transaction per order, in the order given.

A corporate action records a split, a reverse split or a bonus of one holding. On the new transaction page, the kind "Corporate action" takes the brokerage, the investment, the first day without the right and the proportion as "For each" and "Holder gets". A bonus with a cost per unit adds that value to the cost of the holding and records an exempt income. A fraction leaves the position, and its auction cash is recorded when it arrives. An event is voided and recorded again, not corrected. The tool record_corporate_action does the same: kind is split, reverse_split, bonus or unit_tax; the holding is account_id, or brokerage_id with instrument; date is the first day without the right. ratio_from is "For each" and ratio_to is "Holder gets": a split of 1 into 10 is 1 and 10, a reverse split of 10 into 1 is 10 and 1, a bonus of 1 unit for each 10 held is 10 and 1; both go from 1 to 1000000, ratio_to is above ratio_from in a split and below it in a reverse split. unit_cost is the cost per unit the company states for a bonus and needs category_id, the income category. unit_tax takes no ratio: quantity is the units the fund cancelled, below the units held, and amount the income tax withheld in the base currency. The holding must hold units on the day before date. A merger, an incorporation of shares or a ticker change is kind conversion: the whole holding becomes new_instrument, the code received, at ratio_from and ratio_to, and its whole cost moves to it. A spin-off (cisão) is kind spin_off: the holding stays, new_instrument is received at the ratio, and cost_percent, the percent of the cost the notice of the company assigns to the new security, moves to it. new_instrument must be a defined security with the decimal places of the holding; its holding under the brokerage is created when absent. equori records no gain at the exchange: the tax of a merger follows the notice of the company and of the broker. On the screen, the kinds Conversion and Spin-off ask for the security received and the cost moved.

A subscription right (direito de subscrição) of a capital increase is kind right_issue: the holding stays and new_instrument, the code of the right (such as PETR12), is received at the ratio at no cost, as the tax rule sets. A right is sold with record_investment sell, and the whole price of the sale is gain. Kind right_exercise turns the whole holding of a right into new_instrument, the share or the subscription receipt (such as PETR10), at the ratio; amount is the subscription price paid in total, in the base currency, and is the cost of the units received. It is paid from cash_account_id, or from the cash account of the brokerage when absent. A receipt that becomes shares is kind conversion. Rights left unexercised are sold first, or remain until a later version records their expiry. On the screen, the kinds Subscription right and Right exercise ask for the security received; Right exercise also asks for the price paid.

A custody transfer moves units of one holding to another brokerage with their cost and records no result: on the new transaction page, the kind "Custody transfer" takes the giving holding, the receiving brokerage, the date and the quantity. The tool transfer_custody does the same: account_id is the giving Investment line, brokerage_id the receiving Brokerage, date the day the units arrive and quantity the units moved, at most those held on that date. The receiving Brokerage gets the Investment line when it has none. It is refused when the receiving Brokerage is the one that holds the line, when the quantity is above the holding, or when a sale of the holding is recorded after the date (later_sale_exists).

On New transaction, "Other kinds" opens "Buy or sell an investment": the "Side", the "Brokerage", the "Investment", the "Quantity" and the "Unit price", or an "Order total" for an order at several prices, the optional "Settlement" and "Fees"; the form states the "Gross" and "What leaves or enters the account" before "Save". The help page Buy an investment (/help/buy-investment) shows the steps with figures and a video.
