The **Income split** divides the amount to distribute into buckets, such as 50/30/20 (needs, wants, savings) or six buckets for fixed costs, comfort, goals, pleasures, financial freedom and knowledge. It lives in the Budgets tab of the Planning screen.

Each bucket has a name, a share and how it counts. The shares sum to 100%. A bucket counts in one of four ways:

- Spending ceiling: the bucket keeps its target when its categories spend at most the share.
- Minimum to use: the bucket keeps its target when its categories spend at least the share.
- Month's savings: the bucket counts the money moved into the savings, reserve and brokerage accounts that are not goal accounts, and into the accounts of the goals marked as savings. One bucket at most.
- Goal deposits: the bucket counts the money moved into the accounts of the goals from other accounts. One bucket at most.

A movement counts in one bucket: a deposit into a goal account counts in the goals bucket, a deposit into another savings account in the savings bucket, and a withdrawal counts against its month. The destination of the money decides, not the way it was recorded: a salary credited to a savings account is a deposit, and an expense paid from a goal account is a withdrawal, as the same expense after a move to checking is. Yields, realized gains and losses and price changes do not count. A yield or a realized gain that stays in the investments is retained income: it is left out of the income received, so the targets do not rise with it; a yield credited to a checking account stays in the income received. Income not spent and not set aside counts in no bucket: it is the unallocated income of the month, the income minus the expenses by transaction date and the money set aside. With an installment purchase, which counts in full on its purchase date, the unallocated income is not money left in the accounts. The buckets, the spending outside the split and the unallocated income sum to the income received.

Each expense category goes in one bucket. A subcategory follows its category unless it gets a bucket of its own. A category in no bucket counts as spending outside the split.

Setting it up: "Set up split" or "Edit split", in the split section of the Plan view, opens the dialog. A book without a split chooses a model first: 50/30/20, six buckets or from scratch. The dialog then shows the buckets as a bar and as rows, each with its number, name, share, how its target counts and the number of its categories; the sum of the shares is written beside the title, with what is missing or over. "Use a model" replaces the buckets with a model. Below, the categories form a matrix: one row per category, one column per bucket and a last column for the categories without a bucket. A click on a column gives the category that bucket. A category with subcategories opens; a subcategory that follows its category shows the outline of the bucket, and a click on another column gives it a bucket of its own. A plus sign with a number in a column says how many subcategories of the row are in that bucket. The Categories screen opens the same dialog, shows the bucket of each category and sets it in the category form.

Versions: the split can change over time. A change starts in the month chosen in the field "Valid from", beside "Save split" at the foot of the dialog: the start of the version in force, the month on screen or the current month. Earlier months keep the split they had, with their shares and the bucket of each category, so a category can sit in one bucket in one period and in another bucket later. The first version also covers the months before it. A category moved on the Categories screen starts a version in the current month. "End split", at the end of the dialog, ends the split from the chosen month on; earlier months keep it.

History: the History button of the split section, in Plan and in Track, lists each version, the latest first, with its start, the share of each bucket and what changed against the version before: a share, a name, a bucket added or removed, a category moved. The actions menu of a version holds Edit and Delete: Edit opens the dialog on that version. Delete returns its months to the version before.

Plan view: lists each bucket with its share, its target on the amount to distribute, what the budgets of its categories plan and what was spent, and groups the plan by bucket. Below the table, one line states how many open expense categories are outside the split: the ones in no bucket, with no parent category in a bucket. "Place them" opens the dialog, which states how many categories have no bucket above the matrix.

Track view: says how many buckets missed their target in the month. Each bucket shows what is left or over against its share of the income received in that month, and a strip of six months that says whether it kept its target in each one. A tick over a month marks the month in which the split changed. A line states since when the split of the month is in force. Below the buckets, one line states the spending outside the split and one line the unallocated income: the amount that stayed in the accounts, outside every bucket, or the amount paid from balances beyond the income received.

Entries of a bucket: on Track, the name of a bucket opens a panel with the amount counted, the target and the difference, then the entries of the month grouped by category with the subtotal of each group, the largest first. Each entry opens its transaction. A month choice moves over the six months. "See entries" on the line of the spending outside the split opens the same panel.

The assistant reads the split of each month, its versions, the unallocated income and the categories outside the split. It saves a version from a month on, and moves one category to another bucket, or out of every bucket, from the current month on. Every change asks for confirmation.
