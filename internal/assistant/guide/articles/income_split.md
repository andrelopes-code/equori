The **Income split** divides the amount to distribute into buckets, such as 50/30/20 (needs, wants, savings) or six buckets for fixed costs, comfort, goals, pleasures, financial freedom and knowledge. It lives in the Budgets tab of the Planning screen.

Each bucket has a name, a share and how it counts. The shares sum to 100%. A bucket counts in one of four ways:

- Spending ceiling: the bucket keeps its target when its categories spend at most the share.
- Minimum to use: the bucket keeps its target when its categories spend at least the share.
- Month's savings: the bucket counts the money moved into the savings, reserve and brokerage accounts that are not goal accounts, and into the accounts of the goals marked as savings. One bucket at most.
- Goal deposits: the bucket counts the money moved into the accounts of the goals from other accounts and keeps its target when the deposits reach the share. One bucket at most.

A movement counts in one bucket by the destination of the money: a deposit into a goal account counts in the goals bucket, one into another savings account in the savings bucket, and a withdrawal counts against its month. A salary credited to a savings account is a deposit; an expense paid from a goal account is a withdrawal. Yields, realized gains and losses and price changes do not count. A yield that stays in the investments is retained income, outside the income received; a yield credited to checking stays in it. The unallocated income is the income minus the expenses by purchase date and the money set aside: with an installment purchase it is not money left in the accounts. The buckets, the spending outside the split and the unallocated income sum to the income received.

Each expense category goes in one bucket. A subcategory follows its category unless it gets a bucket of its own. A category in no bucket counts as spending outside the split.

Setting it up: "Set up split" or "Edit split", in the split section of the Plan view, opens the dialog. A book without a split chooses a model first: 50/30/20, six buckets or from scratch. The dialog then shows the buckets as a bar and as rows, each with its number, name, share, how its target counts and the number of its categories; the sum of the shares is written beside the title, with what is missing or over. "Use a model" replaces the buckets with a model. Below, the categories form a matrix: one row per category, one column per bucket and a last column for the categories without a bucket. A click on a column gives the category that bucket. A category with subcategories opens; a subcategory that follows its category shows the outline of the bucket, and a click on another column gives it a bucket of its own. A plus sign with a number in a column says how many subcategories of the row are in that bucket. The Categories screen opens the same dialog, shows the bucket of each category and sets it in the category form.

Versions: the split can change over time. A change starts in the month chosen in the field "Valid from", beside "Save split" at the foot of the dialog: the start of the version in force, the month on screen or the current month. Earlier months keep the split they had, with their shares and the bucket of each category, so a category can sit in one bucket in one period and in another bucket later. The first version also covers the months before it. A category moved on the Categories screen starts a version in the current month. "End split", at the end of the dialog, ends the split from the chosen month on; earlier months keep it.

History: the History button of the split section, in Plan and in Track, lists each version, the latest first, with its start, the share of each bucket and what changed against the version before: a share, a name, a bucket added or removed, a category moved. The actions menu of a version holds Edit and Delete: Edit opens the dialog on that version. Delete returns its months to the version before.

Plan view: lists each bucket with its share, its target on the reference income and what the budgets of its categories plan, and groups the plan by bucket. After the buckets come the figures no bucket takes (earlier installments, scheduled savings, goal deposits) and what is left to distribute: the planned column sums to the reference. "Earlier installments" opens a drawer with each charge it sums: the purchase, the card, the installment number or "Anticipation", and the amount. Below the table, one line states how many open expense categories are outside the split; "Place them" opens the dialog.

Track view: says how many buckets missed their target in the month. A strip splits the income received into what was spent in the buckets, set aside, deposited into goals, spent outside the split and left unallocated. Each bucket states its target with its base, the share of the income received, what is left or over, and six months that say whether it kept its target; a tick marks the month the split changed. When the income received differs from the reference of the plan, the line under the title states both. A spending bucket is judged without what the accumulated balance of its budgets paid and what a goal paid, both written under its figure. The goals bucket is judged on its deposits; a withdrawal is written beside them and misses no target. The legend of the strip also writes the retained yields, which take no part of the strip; a line under each figure states its share of the income or its base.

Entries of a bucket: on Track, the name of a bucket opens a panel with the amount counted, the target and the difference, then the entries of the month grouped by category with the subtotal of each group, the largest first. Each entry opens its transaction. A month choice moves over the six months. "Outside the split" in the legend of the strip opens the same panel.

The assistant reads the split of each month, its versions, the unallocated income and the categories outside the split. It saves a version from a month on, and moves one category to another bucket, or out of every bucket, from the current month on. manage_budget_or_goal with delete_bucket_version and month deletes the version that starts in that month; its months fall to the version before it, and a month that starts no version is refused (not_found). get_budgets_and_goals with kind split lists the split of six months and every version; with kind bucket_entries, month and bucket (a bucket name of that month, or none for the spending outside the split) it lists the transactions that add up to the real amount of the bucket: date, category, payee, amount and kind, which is expense, savings or goals. Every change asks for confirmation.

manage_budget_or_goal with save_buckets writes the whole split from month on (the current month when absent): buckets lists every bucket with name, share_bp (basis points; the shares add up to 10000), category_ids (expense categories; their subcategories follow), takes_savings (the bucket also receives the savings; one bucket at most), takes_goals (it also receives the goal deposits) and at_least (the target is a floor, not a ceiling). An empty buckets list removes the split. move_category moves category_id to bucket, a bucket name of the current split, or to none, outside every bucket.
