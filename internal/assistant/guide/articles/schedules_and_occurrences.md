A **Schedule** is a recurring bill, income, **Subscription**, transfer or saving with a **Recurrence rule**, such as monthly on day 5. Each expected date is an **Occurrence**; a date that falls on a weekend or holiday moves by the schedule's business-day rule.

When the transaction that pays an occurrence is recorded, it is linked to the occurrence (realized). A realized occurrence shows the amount received beside the expected one. An occurrence can be skipped or moved, and a skipped occurrence can be reopened: it becomes expected again, and a recorded transaction that fits it is linked. The action Rename on the schedule screen changes the description of the schedule, also after it ended, and the new name applies to every occurrence. The action Start earlier moves the first date of the schedule before the current one, on the rhythm of its recurrence: a new date that is not overdue takes a charge recorded later, and a new date that is overdue is skipped unless a recorded charge fits it. New terms apply from a date; a pause suppresses a period; ending stops future occurrences. When the last 3 realized occurrences each differ from the current expected amount by at least 1% in the same direction, the schedule screen offers Update expected amount, which opens the new terms with the median of the 3 amounts and the date of the next expected occurrence; the terms are saved only after confirmation.

The action Matching settings on the schedule screen sets three values that decide which transaction is linked to an occurrence: the match tolerance (the percentage the amount may differ from the expected amount), the days around the expected date (0 to 31) and the grace days (days after the expected date before the occurrence is overdue). A change applies to transactions recorded from then on; an earlier transaction is linked from the occurrence.

The action Move to another account on the schedule screen, and in the actions menu of a subscription, opens the new schedule form filled with the data of that schedule. Only the account is empty. The new rule starts on the next expected occurrence of the schedule. Saving creates the new schedule and then ends the previous one on that date, so its expected occurrences from then on are removed and the realized ones stay. If the previous schedule cannot be ended, the new one is kept and saving again ends only the previous one. A schedule that has already ended offers no move.

When a purchase repeats every week, month, quarter or year, the assistant suggests a subscription schedule and links the purchase to its first occurrence.

The agenda states how the month closes and what is still to pay. A transfer or a saving counts by where its money goes: out of the accounts to spend from (checking, cash, cards) into a savings, reserve, brokerage or goal account it is an outflow, the way back is an inflow, and a move that keeps both ends on the same side is left out of both. The agenda lists open occurrences with Mark paid or Mark received. Schedules shows what stays free after fixed commitments; Subscriptions ranks them by monthly cost.

Scheduled occurrences show on Transactions and on the Account screen, where the **Projection** uses them. The Overview lists the overdue occurrences first, then those of the next 7 days with the days left, and totals what leaves and what enters; a transfer and a saving are listed and left out of both totals.

A saving or a transfer into an account that several goals share can name one of those goals: marking an occurrence paid sets its amount aside for that goal, and undoing it takes the amount back. The Goals screen opens the form with the goal filled in.

The tools. list_schedules lists the schedules with their terms; with schedule_id, one schedule with the versions of its terms and its pauses; with from and to (31 days when to is absent, at most 366), the occurrences of the period, optionally in one state: expected, overdue, realized or skipped; with month, what is committed to that month. find_recurring finds expenses that repeat on an account and are not scheduled yet. project_balance projects the balance of account_id to a date at most 12 months ahead from its schedules.

manage_schedule creates or changes a schedule; action names the operation.

- create: kind (bill, income, subscription, transfer or savings), description, account_id (the balance account the money leaves or reaches), counterpart_id (the category, or the other account of a transfer or a saving), amount, frequency (daily, weekly, monthly or yearly), interval (every how many units, 1 when absent), day_of_month for a monthly schedule, start (the first occurrence) and until (the last possible date; absent for no end). payee_id is optional. paid_by_transaction_id names the transaction that already paid the occurrence on start.
- update: schedule_id with a new description, an earlier first date in starts_on, or the matching values.
- change_terms: schedule_id, from (the first date of the new terms) and the amount, frequency, interval or day_of_month that change. Earlier occurrences keep the terms they had.
- pause: schedule_id, from and to, the first and last paused dates. end: schedule_id and from, the first date without occurrences.

Matching values, on create and update: match_tolerance_bp is how far the amount of a transaction may differ, in basis points (1000, that is 10%, when absent); match_window_days is how many days from the expected date a transaction may realize the occurrence (7 when absent, 0 to 31); grace_days is how many days after the expected date the occurrence becomes overdue (3 when absent).

manage_occurrence acts on one occurrence, with occurrence_id and action: realize links it to transaction_id, the transaction that paid it; skip skips it, with reason; move gives it another date; unrealize releases the linked transaction, and the occurrence is expected again; reopen brings back a skipped occurrence.

On New schedule, the form asks for the "Kind", the "Description", the "Amount", the "Account" and the "Category", then the "Recurrence" ("Frequency", "Interval", "Day") and when it "Ends"; a sentence under the rule states it in words, and "Create schedule" saves it. "Advanced" holds the "Business-day calendar" and the three match fields. On the Agenda tab of Planning, "Mark paid" or "Mark received" opens the transactions to link, and the actions menu holds "Skip" and "Move". The question mark of the New schedule header opens the help page Create a schedule (/help/schedule).
