Reports read your ledger: totals by **Category** for a period, the balance of an account day by day, money in and out of an account, allocation of your assets, return of an investment account and **Net worth**.

The **Accrual view** counts a card purchase on its date; the **Cash view** counts it when the statement is paid. A statement paid in part counts each purchase in proportion to the payment. get_category_totals takes view accrual or cash (accrual when absent); with view cash and category_id it also lists the card amounts of that category in the period, each with its date, its statement and the payment transaction, at most 366 days.

Category totals are on Categories; an account's balance chart and figures on the Account screen; the month summary on Overview.

The net worth tab of Reports reads the last 6, 12 or 24 months, or the period of the header control. Its cards state the Change in net worth from the day before the period to its last day, in money and percent, and split it into Income minus expenses (a card purchase on its date) and Valuation, exchange and adjustments, the remainder; the split is drawn only when the valuation currency is the books' currency. The table of each month's assets, liabilities, net and change opens from View month by month. A missing price or balance leaves a month unvalued; with no month valued the tab says so.

On the net worth tab, the card Net worth by day draws the net worth at the end of each day of a month beside the month's income and expenses, with their totals and result; its month control picks any past month or the next one, marks the current month with a dot and returns to it with the button Today, a month picked in the monthly chart opens its days; a day picked on the line lists its entries. In the current month the line continues dashed to the month's end; the next month draws only the forecast, from the end of that one. The forecast adds the expected occurrences of the schedules, payments that repeat monthly with a similar amount and no schedule, and the day-to-day income and expenses of up to six previous complete months, adjusted by the pace of the month; a large one-off purchase and its refund stay out. The tooltip of a forecast day states its scheduled, recurring and day-to-day amounts and the likely range. On Overview, the Spending pace figure compares the month's spending up to today with the same days of the previous month, in the accrual view. The Balance of the month chart forecasts the Available accounts from their expected occurrences and from the card statements due in the month; each occurrence ahead is a line on its day, and the tooltip of a day names the occurrences whose labels do not fit.

The Indicators tab reads the last complete month, or the one its month control picks. A card states the Savings rate against the mean of the last 12 months, in percentage points, beside the month's Income, Expense and Result; the current month is incomplete, so its rate is marked in progress and left out of comparisons and charts. A strip places each ratio (savings rate, liquidity in months of expense, committed income, debt to income) among the other months, or states why it cannot. Reserve and debts, Income and expense by month and Savings rate by month are charts; The button View month by month and definitions opens a layer of every figure. A month before the first posting is empty, not zero. Cash view.
