equori imports OFX and CSV statement files into an account. Each file is an **Import batch**; each line is an **External record** that is matched to an existing transaction, creates a new one, or waits for your review when the match is ambiguous. The **Deduplication key** stops a line from being imported twice.

A bank connection through an aggregator imports lines automatically. The aggregator's credentials are added once in Settings, under Integrations. Each bank is then connected on Links with Connect bank: write its name, choose Continue, log in to the aggregator, choose the bank and authorize; no id is copied. A connection is renamed on Links with Rename, so two banks of one aggregator have different names. A bank added at the aggregator's site reaches equori only after these steps. Only you can do both; the assistant cannot handle credentials. After connecting, map each bank account to an equori account and sync. Moving an account's start date earlier imports its history from the new date on the next sync; lines already imported are not imported again.

Import a file on Import statement of the account (for a CSV, the profile sets the separator, header rows, rows under the data, date format, columns, and the column and mark that tell a debit from a credit; Identify columns with AI fills the columns from a masked sample); review a batch on Import batch; on Import batch, a selection of lines in review confirms the suggestion of each probable line, creates their transactions or discards them in one step, and group lines are reviewed one by one; manage connections on Links. A statement file the user attaches to the conversation is imported with import_statement_file: file_id is the id of the [file ...] line of the message, an ofx or csv file, and account_id the account the statement belongs to. The card shows the file name, the account and the line count; a csv file needs the CSV profile of the account, and a line already imported creates nothing. A file with lines dated before the account opens is refused (lines_before_opening, with both dates): on Import statement, a dialog moves the opening date to the first line and imports the file; from the conversation, update_account moves opened_on first, and an opening balance keeps its date, so the earlier lines change the balance. A PDF or an image is not a statement file: the assistant reads it and proposes record_transaction. get_csv_profile returns the CSV profile of an account with account_id; save_csv_profile replaces it with account_id, date_column, date_layout (such as DD/MM/YYYY), decimal_separator ("," or "."), description_column and amount_column, or debit_column and credit_column; delimiter is comma, semicolon, tab or pipe, and amount_sign is as_is or inverted. The assistant chooses the columns from the masked sample of the [file ...] line of an attached csv file.

A bank connection follows an account by the institution's identifier, so a rename at the institution keeps its mapping and history. A line that moves money to a set-aside balance becomes a transfer when one balance answers to its name on its date; otherwise it waits for review with no effect on the balance's yield. To settle it, create the transaction and turn it into a transfer to the right balance, or discard it.

A line that moves money between two of your connected accounts becomes a transfer by itself when both banks name you on their lines; the recent syncs of a connection open in a layer from a button next to the last sync, and the layer lists it with its undo. When only one names you, or neither, it waits for review with the other account's transaction as its candidate. A line from an account you have not connected waits for review: create it and turn it into a transfer to the right account, or discard it. A line whose other side is someone else never pairs.

An account the institution stopped listing is marked on Links and the connection's other accounts keep syncing. Linking its new entry to the same account replaces it, and a line read again creates no second transaction.

A file line with the date and amount of a recorded line that the file no longer holds, under another name or id, waits for review instead of creating a second transaction: discard it if it is the same, create it if there were two. A bank connection line that repeats a line of an imported file waits the same way.

A bank connection line reported for the first time with a date before the account's last reconciled balance waits for review instead of creating a transaction, since that balance did not include it: discard it if it did not move the account, create it if it did.

A bank can list the yield of a reserved balance as a line of the checking account. A yield line of an account with reserved balances is created when the balance the bank reported holds it. A line equal to the yield of a mapped reserve is discarded, since the reserve holds that yield, and can be reopened. Any other waits for review: discard it if a reserve yielded it, create it if the account did. A reserve that is not mapped leaves its yield lines in review; map it on Links.

A withdrawal from a reserve that keeps part of its yield arrives as two lines of the checking account: a release line (Liberação de dinheiro at Mercado Pago), the amount that left the reserve, and the withdrawal line, the amount that reached the account. When the balance the bank reported for the account and the amount it reported for the reserve both confirm it, the pair becomes one transfer: the reserve gives the first amount, the account receives the second, and the difference reverses yield on the system account Yield. The release line is then discarded and can be reopened. Otherwise the release line waits for review: discard it if it did not move the account, create it if it did.

A connection also reads fixed income investments. Each movement waits on Links until you confirm it (application, contribution, redemption, interest) or dismiss it; a confirmation has an undo. get_imports with view investments and connection_id lists each investment the connection reports with its movements: kind (buy, sell, income, tax, amortization or transfer), date, amount, state (pending, confirmed or dismissed) and proposal, which is what a confirmation records: application (the first purchase, which creates the contract), contribution, sale, income, tax_of_sale (recorded with its sale) or none. review_investment_movement decides one movement: confirm, dismiss or undo, with movement_id. The field action carries that choice. The confirmation of an application needs brokerage_id, the Brokerage that will hold the contract; the confirmation of an income needs category_id, the income category, and takes withheld, the tax withheld from the amount of the bank, which is the gross (0 when absent); cash_account_id is needed only when the Brokerage has more than one paying account. The application is confirmed before the other movements of the investment (investment_not_applied). An undo voids the recorded transaction and goes from the latest confirmed movement back (later_movement_confirmed); an undo of a dismissed movement makes it pending again. A movement already decided is refused (investment_movement_resolved). has_terms false means the bank reports no rate: the contract is created without terms, and manage_instrument set_terms adds them.

The tools. get_imports reads the import activity; view names what: batches (statement imports with their counts), records (the lines of batch_id, optionally in one state: received, matched, created, needs_review, discarded or withdrawn), unconfirmed (entries of account_id that no bank line confirmed), connections (bank connections with their sync state and accounts), syncs (the recent syncs of connection_id with the automatic decisions they made) and investments.

manage_imports acts on imports and connections; action names the operation.

- sync: connection_id; reads the bank now.
- map_account: connection_id and external_account_id, the bank account. account_id is the account of the book that receives its lines, and sync_from the first date imported; ignore stops importing that bank account and takes no account_id.
- resolve_record: record_id and resolution. match links the record to transaction_id, a transaction already in the book; create records a new transaction from the line; discard drops the line.
- reopen_record: record_id of a discarded record, which waits for review again.
- undo_decision: record_id of a record a sync decided alone; the decision is reverted and the record waits for review.
- rename_connection: connection_id and name, the new name.
- delete_connection: connection_id. delete_integration: integration_id, allowed only for an integration without connections.

The assistant does not add the credentials of an integration or a new connection: the user does the first in Settings and the second on the Links screen, because a credential never enters a conversation.
