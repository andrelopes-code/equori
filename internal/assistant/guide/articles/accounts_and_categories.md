An **Account** has an **Account class**: asset and liability accounts hold money you have or owe; income and expense accounts are your categories; equity accounts hold opening balances and adjustments. Asset and liability accounts also have an **Account kind**, such as checking, savings, cash, reserve, brokerage, receivable, loan or credit card.

A brokerage is an account kind with one Investment line per security. Its money is in a Cash account inside it or in an account of the same institution; the articles on investments describe how to create it and record a buy (topic investments).

A **Category** is an income or expense account. Categories form a tree: a **Parent account** sums its children, so Food can hold Supermarket and Restaurant. An **Institution** is the bank or broker that keeps an account.

Create accounts and categories on the New account screen: pick a kind from the two groups, What you have and What you owe, read what the form will ask in the panel beside them, then Continue; rename, move, close or reopen them on Edit account. Merging a category moves all its postings into another category and cannot be undone; it is done on the Categories screen. Closing an account keeps its history; reopening brings it back.

The Accounts screen lists every account once. Group by arranges the list by kind, by institution or in one flat list, and Order puts the largest balance first, sorts by name or puts first the accounts with an open reconciliation difference; the browser remembers the last choice. A parent account shows the sum of its sub-accounts and folds them with its arrow. The positions of a brokerage, held in a quantity and not in money, stand in one folded line opened with its own arrow. An account reconciled reads its date, an account in difference reads the amount, and an account never reconciled says so.

An account whose institution has an ISPB identifier gets the tab Institution once an admin enables the IF.data feed on the market data settings and the Banco Central publishes figures for it: Basel index, principal capital, fixed-asset ratio, equity and net income of the prudential conglomerate, each with a quarterly trend, the base date and a link to IF.data. A Basel index or principal capital under the minimum of Resolução CMN 4.958/2021 is highlighted; a blank figure reads Not informed. A phone does not offer the tab.

The month composition chart on the Categories screen ranks the top-level categories by their share of the month. Selecting a category that has children shows the composition of that category: each child and the amount booked on the category itself. The Back to the groups button returns to the top level. The Categories tree hides categories without movement behind the switch Show categories without movement, which names how many it hides.

The assistant can also set the colour and the icon of an account or category, when it creates or changes one, and the identifier of an institution; each change waits for the user's confirmation.
