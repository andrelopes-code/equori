An **Account** has an **Account class**: asset and liability accounts hold money you have or owe; income and expense accounts are your categories; equity accounts hold opening balances and adjustments. Asset and liability accounts also have an **Account kind**, such as checking, savings, cash, reserve, brokerage, receivable, property, loan or credit card. A property account holds a home, a vehicle or another good at the value you state; a new value is a balance update resolved as an adjustment, so it changes the net worth and never the income or the expenses.

A brokerage is an account kind with one Investment line per security. Its money is in a Cash account inside it or in an account of the same institution; the articles on investments describe how to create it and record a buy (topic investments).

A **Category** is an income or expense account. Categories form a tree: a **Parent account** sums its children, so Food can hold Supermarket and Restaurant. An **Institution** is the bank or broker that keeps an account. list_payees_and_tags kind identifier_schemes lists the schemes that identify an institution with the pattern of each, and get_institution_indicators reads the figures the regulator publishes for a scheme and an identifier: the ratios as decimal fractions (0.155 is 15.5%), equity and net income by quarter, newest first, with the minimum of each ratio and the norm that sets it. A figure the source does not report is absent, never zero; an institution the source does not report is not_found.

Create accounts and categories on the New account screen: pick a kind from the two groups, What you have and What you owe, read what the form will ask in the panel beside them, then Continue; rename, move, close or reopen them on Edit account. Merging a category moves all its postings into another category and cannot be undone; it is done on the Categories screen. Closing an account keeps its history; reopening brings it back.

The Accounts screen lists every account once. Group by arranges the list by kind, by institution or in one flat list, and Order puts the largest balance first, sorts by name or puts first the accounts with an open reconciliation difference; the browser remembers the last choice. A parent account shows the sum of its sub-accounts and folds them with its arrow. The positions of a brokerage, held in a quantity and not in money, stand in one folded line opened with its own arrow. An account reconciled reads its date, an account in difference reads the amount, and an account never reconciled says so. On the basis booked, a position and an account in another currency add their value in the base currency, at the stored price of the date, to the total of their group and to the three figures, so the net worth equals the one of Reports; a brokerage row adds the value of its positions. On the basis settled, or when a price is missing, such an account stays out of the total and its group says so. A property reads the date of its last valuation and, under its value, the change since its purchase; a loan reads the share already repaid and the month its installment schedule ends.

The page of a property shows its current value with the date of the last valuation, the change since the purchase, and Your share: the value less what the loans that paid for it still owe, when the purchase was recorded with the loan in the same transaction. Its chart covers the whole life of the good from zero. Revalue records the new value, and the tab Valuations lists them. The page of a loan shows the outstanding debt against the amount financed, the share repaid, the payoff month and the installments left from its transfer schedule with an end date, and the interest paid; its chart draws the debt since the contract.

An account whose institution has an ISPB identifier gets the tab Institution once an admin enables the IF.data feed on the market data settings and the Banco Central publishes figures for it: Basel index, principal capital, fixed-asset ratio, equity and net income of the prudential conglomerate, each with a quarterly trend, the base date and a link to IF.data. A Basel index or principal capital under the minimum of Resolução CMN 4.958/2021 is highlighted; a blank figure reads Not informed. A phone does not offer the tab.

On a phone, the activity of an account reads as a statement: the movements grouped by day, each day with its net change, each row with the category or the other account under its title and the amount at the end, with the balance it left under it. A pending or voided movement names its state; a cleared or reconciled one carries no word.

The month composition chart on the Categories screen ranks the top-level categories by their share of the month. Selecting a category that has children shows the composition of that category: each child and the amount booked on the category itself. The Back to the groups button returns to the top level. The Categories tree hides categories without movement behind the switch Show categories without movement, which names how many it hides.

The assistant can also set the colour and the icon of an account or category, when it creates or changes one, and the identifier of an institution; each change waits for the user's confirmation.

The tools. list_accounts lists accounts and categories with their IDs, institution and the base currency; class keeps one of asset, liability, equity, income or expense, and include_closed adds the closed ones. get_balances returns the balance of each asset, liability and equity account at the end of a date; basis booked places a posting on the transaction date and settled on the settlement date of the institution. get_account_details returns one account in depth: reconciliation state and recorded institution balances and, for a credit card, its plastic cards, limit, cycle and statements. get_account_history returns the balance or the flows of one account over time.

manage_accounts_and_payees changes accounts, categories, payees, institutions and tags; action names the operation.

- create_account: name and class. The class income or expense creates a category. An asset or liability takes kind: checking, savings, cash, wallet, benefit, reserve, brokerage, receivable, property, loan, credit_card or other. Optional: currency (the base currency when absent), parent_id, institution_id, opened_on, color (slot 1 to 8), icon and yield_mode, where auto books the yield of the account and none does not.
- update_account: account_id and the fields that change: name, parent_id, institution_id, color (0 clears it), icon (none clears it), yield_mode, excluded_from_liquidity, and opened_on, which moves only to an earlier date.
- close_account and reopen_account: account_id; close_account takes date, today when absent.
- merge_category: account_id is merged into into_id, which remains.
- create_payee: name. rename_payee: payee_id and name. merge_payee: payee_id is merged into into_id.
- create_institution: name. delete_institution: institution_id. set_institution_identifier: institution_id with scheme and identifier; without both it clears the identifier.
- rename_tag: tag and name. merge_tag: tag is merged into into_tag. delete_tag: tag.

A merge is not undone. Card settings belong to manage_card, not to this tool.
