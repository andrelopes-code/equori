Open funds of a book of region BR for an individual. Lei 14.754/2023 read on 2026-11-18 at planalto.gov.br. The law changes: the notice of the administrator prevails. A listed FII, Fiagro or ETF: get_help topic equities_br.

Subtypes. The subtypes of fund are fixed_income_fund, multimarket, equity_fund, pgbl and vgbl (get_portfolio view subtypes).

Come-cotas. Come-cotas applies to fixed_income_fund and multimarket, and not to equity_fund; it is recorded with record_corporate_action kind unit_tax. A fixed income or multimarket fund cancels units on the last business day of May and of November for the tax on the gain: 15% for a long-term fund, 20% for a short-term one (Lei 14.754/2023, art. 17 to 19). Long term and short term share one subtype, so the user states the rate of the fund.

Pension plans (previdência privada). A PGBL or a VGBL is a fund of subtype pgbl or vgbl at the insurer, with the value of its quota: a contribution is a purchase of quotas with record_investment buy, a redemption a sale with the income tax the insurer withheld as taxes, and a portability to another plan a transfer_custody. There is no come-cotas. The tax of a redemption follows the table the participant chose, which equori does not compute: progressive, with 15% withheld in advance, or regressive by the age of each contribution, from 35% under 2 years down by 5 points every 2 years to 10% over 10 years (Lei 11.053/2004, art. 1; the choice may be made until the benefit or the first redemption and is then final, Lei 14.803/2024). A PGBL is taxed on the whole redemption and its contributions are deducted from taxable income up to 12% of it, for a participant who also contributes to the public pension (Lei 9.532/1997, art. 11); a VGBL is taxed on the gain only and its contributions are not deducted. get_tax_year returns the contributions of the year to PGBL plans as deductible_contributions, and each redemption with the tax class pension_pgbl or pension_vgbl. Lei 14.803/2024 checked on 2026-11-18 at camara.leg.br; the statement of the insurer prevails.
