Open funds of a book of region BR for an individual. Lei 14.754/2023 read on 2026-11-18 at planalto.gov.br. The law changes: the notice of the administrator prevails. A listed FII, Fiagro or ETF: get_help topic equities_br.

Subtypes. The subtypes of fund are fixed_income_fund, multimarket and equity_fund (get_portfolio view subtypes).

Come-cotas. Come-cotas applies to fixed_income_fund and multimarket, and not to equity_fund; it is recorded with record_corporate_action kind unit_tax. A fixed income or multimarket fund cancels units on the last business day of May and of November for the tax on the gain: 15% for a long-term fund, 20% for a short-term one (Lei 14.754/2023, art. 17 to 19). Long term and short term share one subtype, so the user states the rate of the fund.
