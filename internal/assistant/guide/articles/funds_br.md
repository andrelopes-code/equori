Open funds of a book of region BR for an individual. Lei 14.754/2023 read on 2026-11-18 at planalto.gov.br. The law changes: the notice of the administrator prevails. A listed FII, Fiagro or ETF: get_help topic equities_br.

Subtypes. The subtypes of fund are fixed_income_fund, multimarket, short_term_fund, equity_fund, fi_infra, fidc, fip, pgbl and vgbl (get_portfolio view subtypes).

Come-cotas. Come-cotas applies to fixed_income_fund, multimarket, short_term_fund and fidc, and not to equity_fund; it is recorded with record_corporate_action kind unit_tax. Such a fund cancels units on the last business day of May and of November for the tax on the gain: 15% for a long-term fund (fixed_income_fund, multimarket, fidc), 20% for a short-term one, whose portfolio averages a term of 365 days or less (short_term_fund; Lei 14.754/2023, art. 17 to 19). get_portfolio view fixed_income_events gives each come-cotas an estimated_tax: the rate of the subtype on the gain of the units since the later of their average cost and their price at the previous come-cotas, absent without a price; a loss of an earlier period is not deducted, so the administrator's notice prevails.

Equity fund. The redemption of an equity_fund withholds 15% of the gain whatever the days held, and no IOF (Lei 14.754/2023, art. 21; Decreto 6.306/2007, art. 32 §2). get_portfolio gives its position an estimated_tax and an estimated_net for a redemption of the whole quantity at the market value, on the average cost.

Pension plans (previdência privada). A PGBL or a VGBL is a fund of subtype pgbl or vgbl at the insurer, with the value of its quota: a contribution is a purchase of quotas with record_investment buy, a redemption a sale with the income tax the insurer withheld as taxes, and a portability to another plan a transfer_custody. There is no come-cotas. The tax of a redemption follows the table the participant chose, which equori does not compute: progressive, with 15% withheld in advance, or regressive by the age of each contribution, from 35% under 2 years down by 5 points every 2 years to 10% over 10 years (Lei 11.053/2004, art. 1; the choice may be made until the benefit or the first redemption and is then final, Lei 14.803/2024). A PGBL is taxed on the whole redemption and its contributions are deducted from taxable income up to 12% of it, for a participant who also contributes to the public pension (Lei 9.532/1997, art. 11); a VGBL is taxed on the gain only and its contributions are not deducted. get_tax_year returns the contributions of the year to PGBL plans as deductible_contributions, and each redemption with the tax class pension_pgbl or pension_vgbl. Lei 14.803/2024 checked on 2026-11-18 at camara.leg.br; the statement of the insurer prevails.

FI-Infra, FIDC, FIP. A fi_infra fund (Fundo de Investimento em Infraestrutura, of incentivized debentures) has the class fund_exempt: its income and distributions are exempt for an individual (Lei 12.431/2011, art. 3) and it pays no come-cotas. A fidc and a fip have the class fund. A FIDC pays come-cotas at 15% unless it is an investment entity; a FIP pays the tax at a distribution, an amortization or a redemption (Lei 14.754/2023). A listed fund of these kinds is recorded with exchange BVMF. Lei 14.754/2023 as summarized by law firms on 2026-11-18; the notice of the administrator prevails.
