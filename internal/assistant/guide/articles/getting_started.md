equori keeps a double-entry **Ledger** of your money. Every **Account** you own (checking, cash, card, brokerage) and every **Category** of income or expense is an account of the ledger. A **Transaction** moves money between accounts through its **Posting** lines, which always add up to zero.

Start with your accounts and their opening balances on the Accounts screen, then record transactions by hand, by text capture, by importing bank files or by linking a bank. The Overview screen summarizes the month; Transactions lists everything by day.

The assistant reads your data, explains concepts and screens, and prepares changes. Every change it prepares is a proposal you confirm or reject on a card in the conversation; nothing changes before you confirm.

On a wide screen, a destination of the navigation column whose page has tabs (Transactions, Categories, Accounts, Planning, Reports) is one row with an arrow at its end: a press goes to the page and opens its tabs as rows under the destination, each one a direct link to that tab; a press on the row while on that page closes or opens the tabs, and the browser keeps the groups left open. With the column narrowed to icons, the press also lists the tabs beside the icon.

On a wide screen, the key C or the Calculator command of the navigation column opens a calculator that floats over any screen. Type the whole account on one line (for example `1200 * 1,05 - 300`); the result updates on every key, Enter keeps it as the start of the next account, and the copy button puts it on the clipboard. Drag the window by its bar; Escape closes it, and the account stays on it until you clear it.

The Help screen opens with a search: it reads the titles, the sections, the glossary terms and the text of the help pages, ignores accents and case, and accepts one changed letter. The key / focuses the search from any help page; the arrow keys move through the results, Enter opens one and Escape clears. Under the search, Help lists the first-month walkthrough as numbered stops, the pages by area of the application (Accounts, Cards, Transactions, Portfolio, Planning, Installation), the symptoms of the troubleshooting page and the glossary terms. A help page lists its sections beside the text and numbers its steps; a page with figures shows the screen with the place of each step marked, and may offer a short muted video of the steps. The pages with figures are Import a statement, Pay a statement, Check an account's balance, Create a category rule and Connect a bank; the first four offer a video. The video opens in a player over the page, closes with its button, with Escape or with a press outside it, and the focus returns to the button "Watch the video". A browser that does not play WebM VP9 video is offered the figures alone. The button in the page header opens the screen the page describes. A page of steps ends with the symptoms of its task, the related pages and a button that opens the assistant with the page title in the message field.
