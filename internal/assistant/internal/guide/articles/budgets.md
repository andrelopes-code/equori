A **Budget** sets a monthly amount for an expense category from a month on. Each month shows the planned amount, what was carried from the previous month when rollover is on, what was spent in the category and its subcategories, and what remains available.

Budgets use the **Accrual view**: a card purchase counts in the month of its date, not when the statement is paid.

The budgets tab of Planning shows each budget of a month, the spending no budget covers and what the month already commits; a budget's page shows its amounts by month and compares planned and spent. A budget created by mistake is deleted from its card or its page; its category and transactions stay. The assistant also creates and deletes budgets, changes their amounts and reports their progress.
