A **Schedule** is a recurring bill, income, **Subscription**, transfer or saving with a **Recurrence rule**, such as monthly on day 5. Each expected date is an **Occurrence**; a date that falls on a weekend or holiday moves by the schedule's business-day rule.

When the transaction that pays an occurrence is recorded, it is linked to the occurrence (realized). A realized occurrence shows the amount received beside the expected one. An occurrence can be skipped or moved. The action Rename on the schedule screen changes the description of the schedule, also after it ended, and the new name applies to every occurrence. The action Start earlier moves the first date of the schedule before the current one, on the rhythm of its recurrence: a new date that is not overdue takes a charge recorded later, and a new date that is overdue is skipped unless a recorded charge fits it. New terms apply from a date; a pause suppresses a period; ending stops future occurrences. When the last 3 realized occurrences each differ from the current expected amount by at least 1% in the same direction, the schedule screen offers Update expected amount, which opens the new terms with the median of the 3 amounts and the date of the next expected occurrence; the terms are saved only after confirmation.

The action Matching settings on the schedule screen sets three values that decide which transaction is linked to an occurrence: the match tolerance (the percentage the amount may differ from the expected amount), the days around the expected date (0 to 31) and the grace days (days after the expected date before the occurrence is overdue). A change applies to transactions recorded from then on; an earlier transaction is linked from the occurrence.

The action Move to another account on the schedule screen, and in the actions menu of a subscription, opens the new schedule form filled with the data of that schedule. Only the account is empty. The new rule starts on the next expected occurrence of the schedule. Saving creates the new schedule and then ends the previous one on that date, so its expected occurrences from then on are removed and the realized ones stay. If the previous schedule cannot be ended, the new one is kept and saving again ends only the previous one. A schedule that has already ended offers no move.

When a purchase repeats every week, month, quarter or year, the assistant suggests a subscription schedule and links the purchase to its first occurrence.

Scheduled occurrences show on Transactions and on the Account screen, where the **Projection** uses them.
