Your language sets the language of the app and of the assistant's answers. It follows you on every device, as your appearance choices do.

The region of your finances sets how numbers and dates are written and which regional rules apply, such as the business-day calendar and tax rules of investments. The time zone sets which day is today and when each month starts; until one is chosen, the region's zone applies.

The base currency is chosen when your finances are created and can change only before the first transaction. Language model providers, the chain of models of each use and daily limits are in the Assistant section of Settings.

Change the language in Settings, Language, and the region, time zone and base currency in Settings, Finances, or ask the assistant to change language or region.

Settings, Data, exports every record as an archive and restores an archive into finances without transactions. Export with credentials also seals the credentials of the integrations and the keys of the language model providers under a passphrase of 8 to 256 characters that equori does not store. The dialog proposes a generated passphrase of 25 characters; save it before confirming, because it cannot be recovered and uppercase and lowercase letters differ. The owner may replace it with one of their own, typed twice. When importing such an archive, type the same passphrase, or choose to import without the credentials and enter them again in Integrations and in the assistant settings. The providers, the model chains and the daily limits travel with every archive; only the keys need the passphrase. Only an owner exports or imports credentials.
