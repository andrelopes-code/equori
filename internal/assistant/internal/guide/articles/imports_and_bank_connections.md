equori imports OFX and CSV statement files into an account. Each file is an **Import batch**; each line is an **External record** that is matched to an existing transaction, creates a new one, or waits for your review when the match is ambiguous. The **Deduplication key** stops a line from being imported twice.

A bank connection through an aggregator imports lines automatically. Adding the aggregator's credentials and connecting a bank happen on the Links screen and only you can do them; the assistant cannot handle credentials. After connecting, map each bank account to an equori account and sync. Moving an account's start date earlier imports its history from the new date on the next sync; lines already imported are not imported again.

Import a file on Import statement of the account (for a CSV, the profile sets the separator, header rows, rows under the data, date format, columns, and the column and mark that tell a debit from a credit; Identify columns with AI fills the columns from a masked sample); review a batch on Import batch; on Import batch, a selection of lines in review confirms the suggestion of each probable line, creates their transactions or discards them in one step, and group lines are reviewed one by one; manage connections on Links.
