A **Transaction** has a date, a description, an optional **Payee**, optional tags, and two or more **Posting** lines. The **Sign convention**: a positive amount increases an asset or an expense; a negative amount increases a liability, an income or equity. A purchase of 50 on a checking account posts -50 to checking and +50 to the expense category.

A **Split** gives one transaction several categories. A **Correction** creates a new version of the transaction and keeps the earlier one in its history with the reason. Voiding removes a transaction from balances and reports and keeps it in history. A selection on Transactions is voided in one step with one reason; a transaction with reconciled postings is refused and named in the result.

Record transactions on New transaction or Text capture; open one on Transaction detail; change it on Correct transaction. A transaction waiting for a category shows in Transactions with the uncategorized filter. The search box of Transactions matches every word typed, in any order and ignoring case and accents, against the description, the payee and the posting memos. The Amount filter keeps the transactions that move an exact value, at least a value, at most a value or a range of values in the base currency.
