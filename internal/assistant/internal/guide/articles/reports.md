Reports read your ledger: totals by **Category** for a period, the balance of an account day by day, money in and out of an account, allocation of your assets, return of an investment account and **Net worth**.

The **Accrual view** counts a card purchase on its date; the **Cash view** counts it when the statement is paid.

Category totals are on Categories; an account's balance chart and figures on the Account screen; the month summary on Overview.

On the net worth tab of Reports, the card Net worth by day draws the net worth at the end of each day of a month beside the month's income and expenses, with their totals and result, a card purchase on its date; its month control picks any past month, a month picked in the monthly chart opens its days, and a day picked on the line lists that day's entries. On Overview, the Spending pace figure compares the spending of the month up to today with the same days of the previous month, in the accrual view. The Balance of the month chart forecasts the Available accounts from their expected occurrences and from the card statements due in the month; each occurrence ahead is a line on its day, and the tooltip of a day names the occurrences whose labels do not fit.
