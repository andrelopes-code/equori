# Record a transaction

A transaction records money that went out, came in or moved from one of your accounts to another. The [New transaction](/transactions/new) screen records one at a time. The **New transaction** button of the navigation and the N key open the form used last: this one or the text entry.

## Record an expense

![Video: recording an expense, from the kind to the Save button](record-transaction.video)

1. Under **Kind of transaction**, choose **Expense**, **Income** or **Transfer**.
   ![The New transaction form, with the kind of transaction marked](entry-kind)
2. Type the **Amount**.
   ![The form, with the Amount field marked](entry-amount)
3. Under **Pay with**, open the list of accounts.
   ![The form, with the Pay with field marked](entry-account)
4. Choose the account the money left.
   ![The list of accounts, with the chosen account marked](entry-sheet)
5. Check the **Date** and choose the **Category**.
   ![The form, with the Date and Category fields marked](entry-category)
6. Choose **Save**.
   ![The foot of the form, with the Save button marked](entry-save)

The list of accounts has two parts. **Leaves the balance now** holds the accounts that pay at once. **Goes to the statement** holds the credit cards: the expense enters a statement, and the form says which one.

## Income and transfer

- For income, the account field reads **Receive in**.
- For a transfer, choose **From account** and **To account**. The money moves from one of your accounts to another.

## After saving

**Save** opens the list of transactions and shows the notice **Transaction recorded**, with a **View** button. **Save and add another** clears the form and keeps the kind, the account and the date for the next transaction.

**Other kinds** opens the forms for investments, foreign purchases and currency exchange. To record several transactions at once, choose **Write as text**.
