# The month's plan

The plan divides an amount among the budgets of a month. It is in [Planning](/planning), on the **Budgets** tab, in the **Plan** view.

## The amount to distribute

Choose **where the amount comes from**:

- **Expected income**: the sum of the income your schedules expect for the month.
- **Average income**: the average of what came in over the previous months.
- **Fixed amount**: an amount you type.

The bar at the top shows how much of that amount already has a destination and how much is **unassigned**. Installments and scheduled saving enter the bar as commitments.

## Plan each category

Type the planned amount of each category. In **Edit in**, choose **Percent** to type a part of the amount to distribute instead of an amount.

The columns beside it help you decide:

- **Spent in** a month: what the category spent in the previous month.
- **3-month average**: the average of the last three months.
- **Suggested**: the amount the history suggests.

**Suggest from history** fills the categories with the suggestion. **Distribute the rest** sends what is left to one category or splits it in the proportion of the plan.

## Save

**Save plan** records the amounts from the chosen month on. The following months keep the same plan until you change it. Past months are read-only.
