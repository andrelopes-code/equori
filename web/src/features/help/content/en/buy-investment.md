# Record the purchase of an investment

A purchase of an investment takes money from the cash of a brokerage and adds units to the portfolio. It is recorded on [New transaction](/transactions/new), under **Other kinds**.

## Record the purchase

![Video: recording the purchase of an investment, up to the Save button](buy-investment.video)

1. Open **Other kinds**.
   ![The New transaction form, with the kind of transaction marked](trade-others)
2. Choose **Buy or sell an investment**.
   ![The Other kinds menu, marked](trade-menu)
3. Under **Side**, keep **Buy** and check the **Brokerage**.
   ![The form, with the side and the brokerage marked](trade-operation)
4. Choose the **Investment**.
   ![The form, with the Investment field marked](trade-investment)
5. Type the **Quantity** and the **Unit price**.
   ![The form, with the quantity and the unit price marked](trade-quantity)
6. Check the **Gross** and **What leaves or enters the account**.
   ![The summary of the operation, marked](trade-summary)
7. Choose **Save**.
   ![The foot of the form, with the Save button marked](trade-save)

## The fields of the purchase

- Under the **Investment**, the form shows how many units the brokerage holds and which account the money leaves.
- **Settlement** is the day the money leaves. Empty, it follows the settlement cycle of the exchange.
- **Order total** serves an order filled at several prices: the total replaces the unit price.
- **Fees** is what the broker and the exchange charge for the operation, such as brokerage fees.

When the cash of the brokerage does not cover the purchase, the form states how much is short. The **Move money into …** button opens a transfer into that account. The **Save** button stays active.

A sale takes the same steps, with **Sell** under **Side**. The purchase shows in the [portfolio](/help/portfolio).
