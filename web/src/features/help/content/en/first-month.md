# Your first month in equori

This walkthrough goes from your first account to your first report. Each step takes a few minutes.

## 1. Add your accounts

In [Accounts](/accounts), choose **New account**. Pick the kind: bank account, savings, cash, credit card, loan and others. Each kind has a sentence that says what it is for.

Enter the **opening balance**: the balance the institution shows on the day equori starts following the account. For a card, it is the amount owed that day.

Repeat for every account and card you use.

## 2. Record what came in and went out

There are three ways, and you can mix them:

- **Import statement**: on the account's page, send the file your bank offers. See [Import a statement](/help/import-statement).
- **Bank connection**: when your installation has one, transactions arrive on their own. See [Bank connections](/help/bank-connections).
- **New transaction**: record an expense, an income or a transfer between your accounts by hand.

## 3. Give each transaction a category

In [Transactions](/transactions), the **Uncategorized** tab lists what still needs a category. Select several transactions to categorize them at once.

To avoid repeating this every month, create rules. See [Create a category rule](/help/category-rule).

## 4. Check the balances

At the end of the month, compare each account's balance with what the institution shows. See [Check an account's balance](/help/check-balance).

## 5. See where the money went

The [Overview](/) shows the month's balance, spending by category and what needs a decision. In [Reports](/reports), compare months and categories.

Once a full month is recorded, [planning](/planning) starts suggesting budgets from your history.
