# The contribution plan

The [contribution plan](/portfolio/contribution-plan) says where each contribution goes to bring the portfolio to the split you want. The portfolio is split into groups, and each group has a target: the share of the portfolio it should hold. The plan only buys: no investment is sold.

## This month

![The This month block, with the Contribution field marked](plan-month "The amount of the contribution and, beside it, the portfolio today and after the contribution.")

Type the **Contribution** of the month. Beside it, the screen shows the portfolio today and after the contribution. A sentence below says how much goes to each purchase and which groups receive nothing this month.

![The shopping list, with the first purchase marked](plan-list "Each row is a purchase: the asset or the group, the approximate order, the total and the share of the contribution.")

The **Shopping list** holds each purchase with its group, the approximate order in units and the total. **Copy list** copies the purchases.

## Portfolio against the target

![The Portfolio against the target section, with the sentence of the first group marked](plan-ideal "Each group says whether it stands above, below or on its target, with its share today, after the contribution and the target.")

Each group has a bar with its share today, its share with this contribution and the mark of the target. The sentence beside it says whether the group stands above, below or on its target. A group above its target receives nothing this month.

## Edit groups and targets

![The header of the plan, with the Edit groups and targets button marked](plan-edit "The button that opens the editing of the groups.")

**Edit groups and targets** turns the rows into fields. Changing the target of a group adjusts the others in proportion to their targets. A lock keeps a group or an asset fixed while the others move. **Assets (…)** opens the assets of the group. **Save** stores the plan, and **Discard** drops the change.

## Outside the plan

An investment that no group holds shows in the section of investments outside the plan, with the choice of the group that takes it.

Without a plan, the screen explains how it works and offers **Start from my portfolio**, which makes one group per type of investment with its share today, and **Start from scratch**.
