# Record a reimbursement

A reimbursement is money that comes back for an expense already recorded, such as the share of a dinner a friend pays back. Marking the payment received as a reimbursement links it to the expenses it repays and reduces them in the reports.

## Mark a payment received as a reimbursement

![Video: marking a payment received as the reimbursement of a dinner](reimbursement.video)

1. On [Transactions](/transactions), open the payment received with a double click.
   ![The list of transactions, with the payment to open marked](reimbursement-open)
2. In the panel beside the list, open **Transaction actions**, the button with three dots.
   ![The panel of the payment, with its header marked](reimbursement-menu)
3. Choose **Mark as reimbursement**.
   ![The menu of the transaction actions, marked](reimbursement-choose)
4. Check the expense the payment reimburses and the amount in the **Repays** column. The top of the dialog shows how much was received, how much is distributed and how much stays as income.
   ![The Mark as reimbursement dialog, with the list of expenses marked](reimbursement-expenses)
5. Choose **Mark as reimbursement**.
   ![The dialog, with the Mark as reimbursement button marked](reimbursement-submit)

The dialog lists the expenses of the previous 90 days that still have an amount to reimburse. What is not distributed among the expenses stays as income. On a payment without a category, the panel asks **Does this reimburse recent expenses?** and offers **Match to expenses**, which opens the same dialog. When some expenses add up to exactly the amount received, the panel names them.

## From the expense

On an expense, the **Transaction actions** menu offers **Record reimbursement received**. The dialog lists the payments received in the next 90 days with an amount free. Choose the payment and the **Amount reimbursed**.

A reimbursement marked by mistake is undone in the **Mark as reimbursement** dialog of the payment, with **Clear reimbursement**.
