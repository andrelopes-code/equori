# Record transactions as text

The [Write as text](/transactions/capture) screen records several transactions at once. Each line you write becomes a draft: equori reads the amount, the date, the account and the category in it. Nothing is recorded until you confirm.

## Record the lines

![Video: recording two transactions as text, up to the Record button](text-capture.video)

1. Write one transaction per line. A line that names no account uses the **Default account**.
   ![The Write as text screen, with the default account and the text box marked](capture-text)
2. Check the drafts. To change what was read from a line, choose **Edit**.
   ![The drafts of the two lines, marked](capture-drafts)
3. Change what is missing, such as the **Category**, and choose **Apply**.
   ![The dialog of one line, with the Category field marked](capture-category)
4. Choose **Record …**. The button carries the number of drafts. The Ctrl key with Enter does the same.
   ![The foot of the screen, with the Record button marked](capture-record)

Once recorded, a notice says how many transactions went in, with a **View** button that opens the list of transactions.

## How to write a line

A line holds, in any order:

- the amount, such as 230.50;
- the date, such as yesterday, friday or 12/10; without a date, today counts;
- the account or the card, by its name or by the end of its number, such as final 1234;
- the number of installments of a card purchase, such as 3x;
- a tag, after #.

The rest of the line becomes the description. In the draft, the words read are highlighted.

## The drafts

Each draft shows the date, the account, the category and the amount. Beside the category, the draft says where it came from: **by rule**, **by payee history** or **suggested by AI**. A line without an account and without a default account counts among the drafts that need attention, with the reason. Fix the line or choose the default account before recording.

A change made in **Edit** replaces what was read from the line until its text changes.

To record a transaction with every field in view, choose **Use the form**: see [Record a transaction](/help/record-transaction).
