# Create a schedule

A schedule is a bill, an income, a subscription, a transfer or a saving that repeats. Each expected repetition is an occurrence. The occurrences show on [Planning](/planning) and enter the forecast of the month.

## Create the schedule

![Video: creating a monthly schedule, up to the Create schedule button](schedule.video)

1. On [New schedule](/planning/schedules/new), choose the **Kind**: **Bill**, **Income**, **Subscription**, **Transfer** or **Savings**.
   ![The New schedule form, with the Kind field marked](schedule-kind)
2. Type the **Description** and the **Amount**.
   ![The form, with the Description and Amount fields marked](schedule-amount)
3. Choose the **Account** and the **Category**.
   ![The form, with the Account and Category fields marked](schedule-account)
4. Under **Recurrence**, choose the **Frequency**, the **Interval** and the **Day**.
   ![The Recurrence group, marked](schedule-recurrence)
5. Under **Ends**, choose **Never**, **On a date** or **After a number of times**. The sentence below sums up the rule.
   ![The Ends group, with the sentence of the rule, marked](schedule-ends)
6. Choose **Create schedule**.
   ![The foot of the form, with the Create schedule button marked](schedule-create)

## The day of each occurrence

In a monthly schedule, **Day of the month by** sets how the day is counted: **Date**, **Weekday** or **Business day**. **On a weekend or holiday** decides what happens when the date falls on a day without business:

- **Keep the date**;
- **Next business day**;
- **Previous business day**;
- **Next business day within the month**.

Under **Advanced**, the **Business-day calendar** picks the holidays that count. Three fields tune how transactions link to occurrences:

- **Match tolerance (%)**: how far the amount of a transaction may be from the expected one.
- **Days around the expected date**: how many days before or after the date a transaction can be linked.
- **Grace days**: how many days after the expected date the occurrence becomes overdue.

## After creating

The **Agenda** tab of [Planning](/planning) lists the occurrences of the month. On an open occurrence:

- **Mark paid**, or **Mark received** for an income, opens the transactions of the account near the date, to link the one that realized the occurrence.
- The actions menu offers **Skip**: the occurrence leaves the projection and the month totals.
- The same menu offers **Move**: the new date holds even after a change of the schedule's terms.

The **Schedules** tab lists the schedules by kind, with their next occurrence.
