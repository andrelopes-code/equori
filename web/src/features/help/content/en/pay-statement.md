# Pay a statement

A statement gathers the purchases of one card cycle. It closes on a date and is due a few days later.

What each figure of the statement means is in [The statement screen](/help/statement).

## Record the payment

1. Open the card in [Cards](/cards).
2. Choose **Pay statement**, or **Pay** on the statement's page.
3. Choose how much to pay: **Open** pays everything still due; **Minimum** pays the minimum payment. You can also type another amount.
4. In **Paid from**, choose the account the money leaves, and enter the payment date.
5. Choose **Pay statement**.

The payment leaves the chosen account and shows as a credit on the next statement. A payment below the total leaves the rest open, and the screen says how much stays.

## Pay in installments

When the institution splits the statement into installments, choose **Finance** on the statement's page. Enter the number of installments and the amount of each. The open amount becomes installments billed on the coming statements, and the screen shows how much of the total is interest.

## Check against the institution

When the institution's bill arrives, compare its total with equori's. Choose **Reconcile** on the statement's page and type the total the institution shows. When the totals differ, record the institution's facts so the dates and transactions follow its statement.
