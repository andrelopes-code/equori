# The portfolio

The [portfolio](/portfolio) answers three questions: how much you have invested, how much you made and how it performed. It adds the investments and the cash of your brokerage accounts on a date. Savings and reserves are on [Accounts](/accounts). On the **Positions** tab, change the valuation date to see the portfolio of a past day.

## The summary

At the top of the **Results** tab, the date is always today:

- **Portfolio value**: the priced investments plus the brokerage cash.
- **Gain or loss**: market value minus cost, without income. It only becomes a real gain or loss when you sell.
- **Income in …**: dividends, interest and other payments received in the year, net of the tax withheld.

![The summary of the portfolio, with the portfolio value marked](portfolio-summary "The portfolio value opens the summary, beside the gain or loss and the income.")

## Where the money is

On the **Positions** tab, it splits the portfolio by type, currency or institution. The same choice groups the list of investments. The brokerage cash goes below zero when a purchase was recorded without the transfer of the money to the brokerage: that row shows a dash in place of the share, the others add up to 100%, and recording the transfer corrects the balance. Your whole net worth, with banks and savings, is on [Reports](/reports).

## The investments

Below, the list shows the name, the market value, the gain or loss in money and in percent of cost, and the share of the portfolio. Under **Columns**, add what you need:

- **Quantity**: how many units the account holds on the date.
- **Average cost**: the average price paid per unit, across all purchases.
- **Cost**: quantity times average cost: what the position cost.
- **Price** and **Price date**: the latest price recorded up to the date.
- **Account**: the brokerage account that holds the investment.

![The list of investments, with the Columns button marked](portfolio-columns "Columns adds the columns of the list.")

A price older than 7 days shows beside the value. An investment without a price stays out of the total and the percentage: record a price on the investment's page, under [Quotes](/instruments), or import a file of prices.

Record buys, sales and income with the buttons above the list. The **+** button on the row of an investment opens the income form of that investment.

## Results

The **Results** tab opens with the figures at the top and a period bar: 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, This year, 12 months, 2 years, 5 years, From the start, or a range of two dates. The **What to see** select picks all brokerages or one account, and the currency select values the figures in another currency. Four blocks follow:

- **Return**: the return of the period and how many points it stands above or below each index over the same days. Each index is a switch that shows or hides its line. The button **View details and method** opens the reconciliation, the rates, the yield credits, the time-weighted return and the flows by month.
- **Rolling 12-month windows**: the return of 12 whole months to the end of each month, with that of the 12 months to today, the best window, the worst and the share of windows below zero and above each index. The first month and the current month count only when whole.
- **Month by month**: the return of each month by year, with the sum of the year and the accumulated return.
- **Evolution of the portfolio**: the value beside the money contributed, with the opening value, the net contributions, the return and the closing value of the period.

Below, **Return by holding** splits each year among the holdings with one method.

## The year

In [Income and sales](/portfolio/year), the **Gain or loss on sales** tab opens with the result of the year, the amount sold and the number of sales. The amount sold per month helps you check a monthly tax limit, which equori does not compute. The **Income** tab opens with what you received, the tax withheld and the gross amount, shows what came in by month and by investment, and splits the totals by type and tax treatment for your tax return. Every sale and payment is in the lists below.

## The projection

The **Projection** tab shows where the plan leads the portfolio, by age. It needs your birth date, which stays in the profile and in [Settings](/settings). Values are in today's money.

- **Plan**: the age of the withdrawals (**Withdrawals at**), the last age (**Until age**), the **Real return** and the **Inflation**. The real returns **Cautious** and **Favorable**, the return **During withdrawals** (empty, the same one holds) and the **Tax on gain**, paid on each withdrawal, are optional. The line of references shows market rates to compare with and fills no field. The **Withdrawal** is an **Income a month**, net of other income, or a **Rate a year** of the portfolio. The return above the withdrawal stays in the portfolio. A withdrawal age already passed means the withdrawals have started, and the projection counts from today, with no contribution. Values change the projection at once, and **Save plan** stores the plan with its date.
- **Wealth journey**: the recorded value and the projection on one chart. Beside it, the amount needed at the withdrawal age, the share of the way done, the income the plan sustains and the age the income ends, if it does not last. The money contributed and the return run up to the withdrawals. In today's money, the past is corrected for inflation.
- **Scenarios**: the same plan under the cautious, the plan's and the favorable return, with the value at the withdrawal age, the share of the needed amount, the income sustained and the age the income lasts to. It appears once a scenario is typed.
- **Plan against recorded**: the recorded value beside the line of the plan, since the first month of the portfolio. Until the month the plan was saved, the line adds the recorded contributions at the rate of the plan; after it, the planned contribution.
- **Planned contributions**: the monthly contribution since the start, with the changes you planned, up to 40. A contribution before the current month does not change. A change starts before the withdrawals.
- **Year by year**: each year with the age at its end, the contributions, the return, the withdrawals, the money contributed and the value. The table opens with 12 years, and **Show the … years** opens all of them.
