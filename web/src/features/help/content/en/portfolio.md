# The portfolio

The [portfolio](/portfolio) answers three questions: how much you have invested, how much you made and how it performed. It adds the investments and the cash of your brokerage accounts on a date. Savings and reserves are on [Accounts](/accounts). Change the valuation date to see the portfolio of a past day.

## The summary

- **Portfolio value**: the priced investments plus the brokerage cash.
- **Gain or loss**: market value minus cost, without income. It only becomes a real gain or loss when you sell.
- **Income of the year**: dividends, interest and other payments received, net of the tax withheld.

## Where the money is

Splits the portfolio by type, currency or institution. The same choice groups the list of investments. Your whole net worth, with banks and savings, is on [Reports](/reports).

## The investments

The list shows the name, the market value, the gain or loss in money and in percent of cost, and the share of the portfolio. Under **Columns**, add what you need:

- **Quantity**: how many units the account holds on the date.
- **Average cost**: the average price paid per unit, across all purchases.
- **Cost**: quantity times average cost: what the position cost.
- **Price** and **Price date**: the latest price recorded up to the date.
- **Account**: the brokerage account that holds the investment.

A price older than 7 days shows beside the value. An investment without a price stays out of the total and the percentage: record a price on the investment's page, under [Quotes](/instruments), or import a file of prices.

Record buys, sales and income with the buttons above the list.

## How it performed

Under **How it performed**, choose the whole portfolio or one account and a period of up to 366 days. The section opens with one sentence that states the result, such as "In the period, the portfolio returned 4.20%: 104.30% of the CDI". Two comparisons follow, each a pair of bars on one axis with a chip:

- **Against the CDI**: the return of the period beside the CDI over the same days. The chip shows the percent of the CDI, or the difference in percentage points.
- **Against inflation**: the return beside the inflation index. The chip shows the real return.

A region without these indexes shows the return of the period alone. The **In money** panel lists the **Opening balance**, the **Net deposits** (when the period has deposits or withdrawals), the **Gain** and the **Closing balance**. A bar splits the growth into deposits and gain. Notes under the comparisons say what the figures leave out. The closed block **Details and method** holds the rate a year, the percent of the index, the index, the yield credits, the opening and closing values, the time-weighted return, the personal rate and the list of deposits and withdrawals.

## The year

In [Income and sales](/portfolio/year), the **Gain or loss on sales** tab opens with the result of the year, the amount sold and the number of sales. The amount sold per month helps you check a monthly tax limit, which equori does not compute. The **Income** tab opens with what you received, the tax withheld and the gross amount, shows what came in by month and by investment, and splits the totals by type and tax treatment for your tax return. Every sale and payment is in the lists below.
