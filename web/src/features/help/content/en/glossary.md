# Glossary

The terms on equori's screens, in alphabetical order.

**Amount to distribute**: in the month's plan, the amount the budgets divide among themselves. See [The month's plan](/help/budget-plan).

**Average cost**: the average price paid per unit of an investment, across all purchases.

**Bank connection**: the access that brings an institution's accounts and transactions without importing files. See [Bank connections](/help/bank-connections).

**Budget**: a monthly amount for a category, compared with what the month spent on it.

**Category**: the reason for an expense or an income, such as Groceries or Salary. A category can hold others inside it.

**Cleared**: the state of a transaction the institution has confirmed. The opposite is pending.

**Closing date**: the day the statement stops taking purchases. A purchase after it goes to the next statement.

**Due date**: the day the statement must be paid.

**Gain or loss now**: how much an investment has gained or lost so far, comparing its market value with its cost.

**Gain or loss on sales**: how much the sales of investments in a year made or lost against their cost.

**Goal**: a balance you want to reach in some accounts by a date.

**Installment plan**: a purchase split into installments, one per statement.

**Link**: the tie between an account a bank connection brings and your account in equori that receives its transactions.

**Match**: to tie an imported line or an expected occurrence to a transaction that already exists, so the same thing is not recorded twice.

**Net worth**: what you own minus what you owe.

**Occurrence**: one expected repetition of a schedule. It is realized when a transaction is linked to it.

**Opening balance**: an account's balance on the day equori starts following it.

**Payee**: the shop, company or person on the other side of a transaction.

**Pending**: the state of a transaction the institution has not confirmed yet, such as a card purchase that was only authorized.

**Reconciled**: the state of a transaction included in a balance check that matched. It changes only after the reconciliation is unlocked.

**Reconciliation**: the comparison of equori's balance with the balance the institution shows. See [Check an account's balance](/help/check-balance).

**Refund**: the return, in full or in part, of a card purchase. The amount comes back as a credit on the statement.

**Schedule**: a bill, income, subscription, transfer or saving that repeats. Each expected repetition is an occurrence.

**Statement**: the purchases of one credit card cycle, billed at once. See [The statement screen](/help/statement).

**Tag**: a free label that cuts across categories, such as a trip. A transaction can carry several.

**Transaction**: a movement of money: an expense, an income or a transfer between your accounts. See [How equori records money](/help/how-money-is-recorded).

**You own** and **you owe**: the sum of the balances of the accounts with money and investments, and the sum of what debts and cards owe.
