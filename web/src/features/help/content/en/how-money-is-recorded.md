# How equori records money

Money never appears or disappears: it leaves one place and enters another. equori records every transaction that way.

## Accounts and categories

An **account** holds money or debt: a bank account, a card, a reserve, a loan. A **category** says where the money went or where it came from: Groceries, Transport, Salary.

A transaction ties the two sides:

- A purchase of 120 at the grocery store with the card: 120 leaves the card and enters Groceries. The card's debt grows.
- A salary of 5,000: 5,000 leaves Salary and enters the bank account.
- A transfer of 500 to the reserve: 500 leaves the bank account and enters the reserve. No category changes, because the money is still yours.
- The statement payment: it leaves the bank account and enters the card. The card's debt shrinks.

Since every amount that leaves enters somewhere else, the balances of the accounts and the totals of the categories always add up.

## What this changes for you

Most of the time, nothing: you choose the account, the amount and the category, and equori builds both sides. That is why a transfer does not show as spending, and why paying the statement does not count the same purchase twice.

The **Advanced** kind of a [new transaction](/transactions/new) shows both sides as lines, called postings. It is for a transaction with several accounts and categories, and its lines must add up to zero.

## Corrections

A corrected transaction keeps its earlier versions in its history. A voided transaction stays in the history and leaves every balance and report.
