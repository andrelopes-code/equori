# The statement screen

A statement's page shows what it bills, how the total was built and what is still due. It opens from the card in [Cards](/cards).

## The figures

- **Previous balance**: what the previous statement left owed.
- **Purchases**: what was charged in this cycle.
- **Credits**: refunds and other credits of this cycle.
- **Payments**: what was paid in this cycle.
- **Total**: the card's balance at closing. The sum is: previous balance + purchases − credits − payments.
- **Open**: what the payments made after closing have not covered yet.

## The states

A statement can be **open** (still taking purchases), **closed** (waiting for payment), **paid**, **partly paid**, **overdue** or **financed**.

A purchase marked **Provisional statement** was made in the last days before closing, and the institution has not yet confirmed which statement it falls on. It may move to the next statement.

## The statement's transactions

The list shows each purchase, credit and payment of the cycle. An installment shows as 3/10: the third of ten. A transaction marked **No bank line** has not been confirmed by the institution yet.

When the institution's total differs, the screen can show purchases made close to the closing date that the institution likely billed on the next statement, with the option to move them.

## The actions

- **Pay**: see [Pay a statement](/help/pay-statement).
- **Finance**: turns the open amount into installments on the coming statements.
- **Reconcile**: compares the total with the institution's.
- **Institution's facts**: records the total, the minimum payment, the closing date and the due date the institution reports. These facts win over the calculated ones.
