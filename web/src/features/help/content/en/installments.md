# Record a purchase in installments

A purchase in installments splits the price into installments, one per statement of the credit card. The first installment enters the statement of the purchase date. The purchase is recorded on the card's page.

## Record the purchase

![Video: recording a purchase in installments, from the card to the Record purchase button](installments.video)

1. On [Cards](/cards), choose the card.
   ![The list of cards, with the card to open marked](installments-card)
2. Choose **Purchase in installments**.
   ![The card's page, with the Purchase in installments button marked](installments-open)
3. Type the **Price** and check the **Purchase date**.
   ![The Purchase in installments dialog, with the price and the date marked](installments-price)
4. Choose the **Category** and the number of **Installments**. Enter the **Interest** when the purchase carries interest.
   ![The dialog, with the category, the installments and the interest marked](installments-count)
5. Choose **Record purchase**.
   ![The dialog, with the Record purchase button marked](installments-record)

Under **Installments**, the dialog shows the approximate amount of each installment and the total. The **Interest** is what is paid beyond the price. A purchase without interest keeps zero.

## After recording

The **Installment plans** tab of the card's page lists each purchase in installments, with the **Installment**, the installments **Billed** and the amount **Still to bill**. The button at the end of the row opens the installments of the purchase. Two actions stand there:

- **Cancel plan**: the installments not yet billed are credited back to the card. The billed ones are refunded as the bank decides.
- **Bring forward**: the remaining installments move to one statement.

A purchase in installments can also be written on a line of [Write as text](/transactions/capture), with the number of installments followed by x, such as 10x.
