# Bank connections

A bank connection brings an institution's accounts and transactions without importing files. It depends on an outside service, called an aggregator, that the person who administers the installation sets up in [Settings](/settings).

## Link the accounts

A connection brings the accounts the institution holds. Each needs a link: your account in equori that receives its transactions.

1. Open [Links](/accounts/links).
2. On each account of the connection, choose **Link**.
3. Choose your account, or create one, and the date transactions are imported from.
4. Choose **Link and sync**.

An account you do not want to follow can be ignored.

## Sync

A sync fetches the new transactions and matches them to the ones you already recorded, as an [imported statement](/help/import-statement) does. Each sync creates an import, with the state of each line.

## When the connection needs you

Sometimes the institution asks for an action only the account holder can take, such as signing in again. The connection shows **Needs you**, and the text says where to complete the action. Then sync again.

## Stop and delete

**Stop syncing** takes an account out of the sync. **Delete connection** removes the connection and its links. Either way, what was already imported stays in your records.
