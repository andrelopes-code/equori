## In Brazil

The aggregator is Meu Pluggy. Personal use is free, without a time limit, for up to 5 connections of one holder. The steps of the aggregator, such as renewing consent, are taken in Meu Pluggy.

## Create the credentials at Pluggy {#pluggy-credentials}

Do this once. Meu Pluggy holds the banks; the Pluggy Dashboard gives the credentials. Use the same account in both.

1. Create the account at meu.pluggy.ai and connect your banks. Each bank asks for approval in its own app.
2. Log in to dashboard.pluggy.ai with the same account and create an application.
3. If the application uses its own choice of connectors, open Customization, Connectors, and turn on MeuPluggy.
4. Copy the Client ID and the Client Secret of the application. The Client Secret is shown only once.
5. Add both in [Settings](/settings), under **Integrations**.

## When it does not work {#pluggy-mistakes}

- The bank is not in equori. Adding it in Meu Pluggy is not enough: choose **Connect bank** in equori.
- The screen asks for the CPF and the bank's approval again. equori opens the MeuPluggy connector only. If this happens in the Pluggy Dashboard, the bank's own connector was chosen.
- The bank is not listed in Meu Pluggy. Connect it at meu.pluggy.ai and return to equori.

When a bank cannot be connected, [import a statement file](/help/import-statement) instead.
