# Web interface rules

These rules apply to `web/`. Paths are relative to `web/` unless they start with `docs/`. The component library is [Slotted](https://github.com/andrelopes-code/slotted) (`@slotted/react`); its guidelines ship in `node_modules/@slotted/react/dist/docs/`.

## Rules

File names under `web/src` are kebab-case (O1). This is the only exception to the snake_case rule of Naming in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

The application sets `data-slotted-theme="equori"` and loads `@/theme/equori.gen.css` in place of the `default` theme and `@slotted/themes/default.css` of the Slotted UI section.

## Screens

These rules replace the Slotted guideline they name; the owner review of 2026-10-21 (`docs/plans/2026-10-21-web-owner-review.md`) set them.

- **Page width (replaces CP-07).** Every destination, list, record and overview page is `full`; a create or edit form is `wide`; the login page is `content`. Moving between destinations never changes the page's measure.
- **Domain controls before families.** Money, a period, a month, a day and an account are what this application is about. Each has a control of the app's own (`ui/money/money-input`, `ui/navigation/period-control`, `ui/navigation/month-control`, `ui/form-fields/date-shortcuts`, `transactions/account-field`); a screen that asks for one of them uses it, and a new screen that meets another such value builds its control under CP-04 rather than settling for a family's generic field.
- **A page reads its own width.** The assistant pane beside the page narrows it while the viewport stays the same (1280 px of viewport leave 676 px of page). A layout of page content changes by `@container slotted-page (...)`, the container of the Slotted `Page`, never by `@media (min-width)` or `(max-width)`; code that picks a layout from 1024 px calls `usePageWide()` (`ui/runtime/use-page-wide`). `useMediaQuery(wideQuery)` and `compactQuery` stay for what follows the shell or the device: the way back, autofocus, a drawer's side, the phone routes. Content of a dialog or a drawer has no `slotted-page` ancestor and keeps its own container.
- **Grids.** A data-grid or table is `variant="framed"` on the page ground. A table that is the main content of a surface section or a card runs edge to edge in it with `variant="ruled"` and `ui/display/section-bleed.module.css`: no card inside a card (owner review of 2026-11-15). An `actions` column is pinned at the end, and a grid holding buttons sets a `rowHeight` that leaves the cursor ring visible around them.
- **Measure on the production build.** A performance check runs on `make run-prod` at 390 px with the CPU throttled 4×; the development build of React costs about 4.5 times as much (one selection: 12 840 ms against 2 840 ms).
- **Forms.** A form page, drawer or dialog follows `docs/contributing/forms.md` (F1 to F14): one column of at most 40rem through `ui/form-layout/form-layout`, a width per expected answer, sections with a legend, a question above every set of choices.
- **Phone screens.** Only the routes of ADR 027 (S3) are served below 600 px; any other route is desktop-only, holds no phone code and is tested at desktop width (S7). Below 600 px a phone route follows `docs/contributing/mobile.md`: a table where the reader compares, `DataList` where the reader works on a record, the records before the figures, and tabs directly above their panel.
- **Headers.** A destination's header carries a subheading that says what the page is for, its period control when the page reads a period, and its primary action. The page header, the page's own, keeps its rule. A header inside the body (a section, a settings pane) draws none: the cards under it separate it.
- **Action weight.** A page has at most one `solid` action, its primary, in the page header. A secondary action is `outline`; a link to another view and a utility of a section (columns, details) are `ghost`. A filter or a value control is never `solid`. In the page header the controls come first, then a vertical `Divider`, then the actions from the lightest to the primary (owner review of 2026-11-15, `docs/specs/2026-11-15-portfolio-panels-design.md`).
- **Header controls carry no visible label.** A field in `PageHeaderActions` (a currency, a date, a year) has no `FieldLabel`; it names itself through `aria-label`, `label` or the family's `messages`, as `portfolio-page.tsx` does.
- **A chart under a title draws no caption.** A chart inside a widget, section or card whose heading already names it takes no `title`; it takes `aria-label` with that heading's text. A caption that repeats the heading ("Balance of the month", then "Balance") is noise.
- **Previous and next.** A pair of steps (a period, a month, a statement) is one `ButtonGroup` (`variant="outline"`, `tone="secondary"`) of two `IconButton`s, or `IconButtonLink`s when each step is a route, drawing `StepGlyph` (`ui/navigation/step-glyph`) with `direction="previous"` and `"next"`. A missing neighbour is a disabled `IconButton` in its place, never an absent one.
- **A name and its tag share a line.** In a table cell a name and the `Tag` that qualifies it sit in one `Inline` (`align="center"`, `wrap="wrap"`); a `Stack` that puts the tag under the name wastes the row's width on a wide screen.
- **Tags keep their distance.** An `Inline` that holds a `Tag` beside text, a link or another `Tag` uses `gap={4}`, in any screen; at `gap={2}` or below the row reads as one block (owner review of 2026-10-29).
- **One minus sign.** A number on screen is written through `src/i18n/format.ts` (`useMoneyFormat()` for an amount, `numberFormatter` for any other number), never through `new Intl.NumberFormat(...).format`: Intl writes U+002D, the app writes U+2212. Text a field reads back (`formatPlain`) keeps U+002D.
- **Way back.** A page below a destination draws its way back with `PageBack` (`ui/navigation/page-back`), never `PageHeaderBack` (ESLint). From 1024 px it sits above the title; below, the shell's top app bar carries it and the page draws none.
- **Alert actions.** The actions of an `Alert` go in the Slotted `AlertActions`; it draws every `Button` in the alert's tone at `sm`. The primary action is the default `solid` Button and every other action is `variant="ghost"`; a lone action is the primary. A button inside an alert sets no `size` or `tone` and is never `outline`: an outline Button draws no fill, so the alert's ground shows through it.
- **Items inside an overlay.** A dialog and a drawer sit at the raised step, so an item drawn from `--slotted-surface-raised` and `--slotted-border-subtle` melts into the panel (1.00:1 and 1.3:1). Slotted families inside one read a wash and a ring of the text colour from `web/src/theme/overlay-items.ts`: 3% and 14%, about 1.06:1 and 1.5:1 of the panel, since a frame is a large area. An element of the app's own that is small and repeated, such as a row, takes more: the text colour at 8% for the ground and at 22% for the edge (`color-mix` in a co-located `*.module.css`, as `account-row.module.css` does). A new family that draws a ground inside an overlay gets a block in `overlay-items.ts` and a case in `equori.theme.test.ts`.
- **Writes refresh.** Every successful mutation marks all queries stale (`api/query-client.ts`); a screen never needs a reload to show the result of a write.

## Assistant conversation

The owner review of 2026-11-15 set these rules for everything the assistant draws in a conversation (`features/assistant`). A new kind of answer block, proposal or result meets all of them before its first commit.

- **A form per kind.** Each kind of information or operation has the form of what it represents (table below). A list of fields is the fallback for a kind the client does not know yet, never the design of a new kind. A new kind adds its row to the table in the same change.
- **One decision per answer.** The proposals of one answer form one sheet: the deadline appears once, one button confirms the marked operations, an operation that waits for another is drawn under it and marked with it. An irreversible operation starts unmarked, carries the word "irreversible", and turns the confirm button to the danger tone when marked.
- **A result replaces its proposal.** An executed write becomes a receipt with its amount first and a link to its record; a failed one states what was not applied and why; an expired one is dimmed and says how to ask again. Status is a word with an icon, never a colour or a badge alone.
- **One thread.** Every assistant turn hangs from the assistant's mark and its rail; sources consulted fold into one line with their icons. No block floats between turns.
- **Motion with a function.** While the turn runs, the mark carries the composer's ring and each step enters with its source icon; on arrival, bars grow from zero and lines draw; a confirmed operation draws its check. Every animation stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`. No gradient text.
- **Honest values.** Every figure comes from the API or from a client derivation named in the spec with its own test (a difference between periods, the next dates of a schedule).
- **Mock first.** A new kind ships with a mock of its form in the light and dark schemes and at 390 px, approved by the owner before code (skill `redesign-ui-section`). The approved mock is archived in `docs/superpowers/mocks/<spec file name>/`, a local folder outside Git; the spec cites that path.
- **The approved mock is the contract.** The code draws every element, state, mark and spacing of the mock. A family that draws less is replaced by an element of the app's own; a simplification is a defect, not a choice.
- **Icons with care.** One geometry (Lucide, stroke 1.75) at one size per text size: 14 px beside 12 to 13 px text, 16 px beside 14 to 15 px text. An icon sits centred on the first line of its text, never on the block. A failure is written in words in the danger tone; a circled cross beside a sentence is not used. An icon that repeats what its word says is dropped.
- **Inner space.** A block keeps at least 12 px between its edge and its content, a sheet 16 px, and its inner padding is never smaller than the gap between its rows. A row has a fixed height that leaves 8 px above and below its tallest line. Before a commit, the screen is captured at 390 and 1440 px in both schemes and no element shows content wider or taller than its box.

## Utility classes

Tailwind v4 utilities (ADR 032) serve a spacing tweak on one element (`<Text className="ms-2">`). `src/app/tailwind.css` is their only configuration.

- The number of a spacing utility is the Slotted token: `ms-2` is `--slotted-space-2`. Tailwind's `ms-2` is 0.5rem; here the step is a token, not a multiple of 0.25rem.
- Steps are `0` and `1` to `12`. A step outside the scale emits no rule.
- Only the utilities exist. There is no palette, type scale or radius scale: a colour, a font size and a corner come from the family's props and from the tokens (O5).
- An arbitrary value (`mt-[13px]`) is forbidden. `src/testing/tailwind-source.test.ts` enforces it.
- A rule of two or more properties, or one that reads any other token, goes in a co-located `*.module.css` (O5).
- A utility goes on an element the app draws or on a family's root through `className`. It never targets a family's parts (O5).

## Slotted workarounds

A defect whose cause lies in Slotted is reported as an issue in `andrelopes-code/slotted`, and the workaround in `web/` (code, CSS, theme rule or test) carries a comment that cites it: `Workaround for Slotted issue #N: <why the app does this>`. `src/testing/slotted-issues.test.ts` checks the form. When an installed Slotted version fixes the issue, the workaround, its comment and any test that exists only for it are removed in one commit whose body names the issue.
