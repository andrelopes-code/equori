# Currencies

This guide states how equori stores, adds, converts and shows amounts. Each rule cites the function that implements it and a test that covers it. `docs/contributing/feature-design.md` C1 to C4 cite this guide. Paths are relative to the repository root.

| ID | Rule | Implementation | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | An amount is an `int64` of units of its instrument's scale, with the instrument code and the scale. A currency takes the minor units of ISO 4217; another instrument takes a scale from 0 to 12. `\|amount\| ≤ 2^53 − 1` (`money.MaxAmount`). Never `float64`. | `money.New`, `money.NewOf` in `internal/platform/money/money.go` | `TestNew`, `TestNewOf` in `internal/platform/money/money_test.go` |
| M2 | A sum or a difference holds one instrument. Arithmetic across instruments returns `money.ErrCurrencyMismatch`. | `Money.Add`, `Money.Sub` in `internal/platform/money/money.go` | `TestArithmetic` in `internal/platform/money/money_test.go` |
| M3 | A conversion multiplies by every leg exactly and rounds half to even once, to the minor units of the target currency. A conversion between two currencies without a leg is an error. A share (`Fraction`) and a unit cost (`Quotient`) round half to even. | `money.Convert`, `money.Fraction`, `money.Quotient` in `internal/platform/money/convert.go` | `TestConvert`, `TestConvertRejects`, `TestFraction`, `TestQuotient` in `internal/platform/money/convert_test.go` |
| M4 | A rate is a positive exact decimal of at most 12 decimals; the API carries it as a decimal string. | `money.ParseRate`, `money.NewRate` in `internal/platform/money/rate.go` | `TestParseRate`, `TestNewRate` in `internal/platform/money/rate_test.go` |
| M5 | A conversion at a date takes the latest stored price on or before that date: a direct price, else the inverse price, else two legs through the instrument priced against both sides whose older leg is the most recent. A price has no maximum age. The same instrument converts to itself without a price. | `Module.Convert` in `internal/marketdata/convert.go` | `TestConvert`, `TestConvertThroughIntermediate`, `TestIntermediateChoice`, `TestIntermediateOldLeg` in `internal/marketdata/convert_test.go` |
| M6 | A conversion without a path returns an error wrapping `marketdata.ErrPriceMissing`. Each consuming module wraps it in its own `ErrPriceMissing`, and the HTTP API answers 422 `price_missing`. | `missing` in `internal/marketdata/convert.go`; `pricesAdapter.Convert` in `internal/ledger/ledger.go`; `pricesPort.Convert` and `ledgerPort.ValuedBalance` in `internal/planning/planning.go`; `marketPort.Convert` and `ledgerPort.ValuedBalance` in `internal/portfolio/portfolio.go`; `priceMissing` in `internal/reporting/reporting.go` | `TestNoPath` in `internal/marketdata/convert_test.go`; `TestS17CurrencyExchange` in `internal/ledger/currency_test.go` (field `value_in`); `TestIndicatorsPriceMissing` in `internal/reporting/indicators_test.go`; `TestAllocationErrors` in `internal/portfolio/allocation_test.go`; `TestGoalWithoutPriceIsNotStored` in `internal/planning/goals_test.go` |
| M7 | A figure that converts names its rates: the response carries the prices it used, each with its date. | `Conversion.Prices` in `internal/marketdata/convert.go`; `Service.Positions` in `internal/portfolio/app/service.go`; `Service.Committed` in `internal/planning/app/views.go` | `TestS66ForeignSubscriptionCommitted` in `internal/planning/currency_test.go` |
| M8 | The date of the rate is the date of the figure: a balance valued in another currency takes the balance date; a position, the allocation and net worth take the report date; a flow of a return takes the flow date; a committed or planned occurrence in another currency takes today (P8 amendment A72). | `Service.ValuedBalance` in `internal/ledger/app/currency.go`; `Service.Positions` in `internal/portfolio/app/service.go`; `Service.valuedBalances` in `internal/portfolio/app/allocation.go`; `Service.valueIn` in `internal/portfolio/app/recorded.go`; `Service.Committed` in `internal/planning/app/views.go` | `TestS17CurrencyExchange` in `internal/ledger/currency_test.go`; `TestPositionsValuedInAChosenCurrency` in `internal/portfolio/positions_test.go`; `TestReturnValuedInAnotherCurrency` in `internal/portfolio/return_test.go`; `TestS66ForeignSubscriptionCommitted` in `internal/planning/currency_test.go` |
| M9 | A figure across currencies is stated in the book's base currency unless the request names another (`value_in`). The base currency comes from the book; at provisioning a region proposes one (`DefaultBaseCurrency`), and a region without an entry requires the user to choose. The cost basis of a position stays in the base currency when its market value is asked in another. | `DefaultBaseCurrency` in `internal/ledger/region_defaults.go`; `Service.Positions` in `internal/portfolio/app/service.go` | `TestProvisioningUsesTheChosenCurrency` in `internal/ledger/provisioning_test.go`; `TestPositionsValuedInAChosenCurrency` in `internal/portfolio/positions_test.go` |
| M10 | A missing price is stated, never read as zero and never dropped in silence. A position whose instrument has no price in the base currency is listed without `market_value`. Any other converted figure without a price fails with 422 `price_missing` (M6). Divergence, issue #43: the trailing return of a goal is absent without a reason when a price is missing; the intended rule gives the absence its reason `price_missing`. | `Service.Positions` in `internal/portfolio/app/service.go`; `Service.TrailingReturn` in `internal/portfolio/app/return.go` | `TestPositionsFollowLedgerChanges` in `internal/portfolio/positions_test.go`; issue #43 adds the test of the intended rule |
| M11 | A total that does not convert holds one currency. The accounts page totals a group over the accounts in the base currency and counts the others as excluded; a parent's subtree adds only the children in the parent's currency; the agenda gives one net per currency, ordered by the currency code. | `groupBalances` in `web/src/features/accounts/balance-groups.ts`; `sheetRows` in `web/src/features/accounts/balance-sheet.ts`; `netOf` in `web/src/features/planning/agenda.ts` | `web/src/features/accounts/balance-groups.test.ts` ("lists foreign balances but excludes them from the total"); `web/src/features/accounts/balance-sheet.test.ts` ("adds to a subtree only the children in the parent's currency"); `web/src/features/planning/agenda.test.ts` ("gives one total per currency, ordered by the currency code") |
| M12 | An amount travels as an integer and an instrument code and is written through `useMoneyFormat()`, which formats by the active tag and masks the amount in privacy mode (`docs/contributing/web.md`, O7, O9). The currency comes from the record or from settings, never from a literal fallback (O10). | `useMoneyFormat` in `web/src/privacy/use-money-format.ts` | `web/src/privacy/use-money-format.test.tsx` ("formats like the locale while visible", "writes the mask in place of every form of the amount while hidden") |
