# Overview (roadmap item 7)

Route `/` renders `OverviewPage` in `web/src/features/overview/`. The month is the month of `useToday()`. Every block loads, fails and stays empty on its own (`overview-block.tsx`). Every amount goes through `useMoneyFormat()`; the currency is the base currency of the settings. Region-dependent values: none; months and dates come from `Intl` with the active locale (ADR 016).

Contract adjustments: the roadmap names `listBalances`, `getBalanceSeries` and `getAccountFlows`; the forecast and month flows use `useOverview`, which reads `/reports/balances`-based series, `/accounts/{id}/projection` and `/transactions`. `/portfolio` does not exist, so the net worth block links to `/accounts`.

## Slice 1: net worth, month flows, spending

- `net-worth.ts`: change of each figure against the end of the previous month (S129 figures).
- `top-categories.ts`: the five largest own totals of `getCategoryTotals`.
- `net-worth-block.tsx`, `month-flows-block.tsx`, `spending-block.tsx`, `attention-block.tsx` (heading only until slice 3).
- Tests: `net-worth.test.ts`, `top-categories.test.ts`, `overview-page.test.tsx`.

## Slice 2: upcoming, cards, budgets, goals

- `upcoming.ts`: overdue occurrences plus those expected in the next 7 days.
- `open-statements.ts`: statements with status `open`, `closed`, `partially_paid` or `overdue` and an unpaid amount.
- `budget-alerts.ts`: `budgetState` of `features/planning` (over, near at 90 % of planned plus carried).
- Blocks for each, with `OccurrenceActions` on upcoming rows.

## Slice 3: attention list

- `attention.ts`: counts of uncategorized transactions, unconfirmed records, connections in `needs_owner` or `failed`, and open accounts with an open reconciliation difference.
- Unconfirmed records and reconciliation differences are one row per account, linking to the account (its reconciliation tab, or the card page for a card). Connections link to `/accounts/links`, the route that exists.
- Empty list: the block is not drawn.
