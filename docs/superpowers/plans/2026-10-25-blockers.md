## Known defects to fix inside the step that touches the file

## Rules for every step

- A behavior change starts with a test that fails without it (CLAUDE.md, Tests).
- A new operation in `api/openapi.yaml` has an entry in `internal/server/tool_exposure_test.go`.
- No regional value outside the implementation of its region (`docs/contributing/regions.md`).
- An ADR amendment is a new row or section in the ADR, dated, with its reason; applied decisions are not rewritten.
