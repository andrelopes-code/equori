# Budgets (web roadmap item 6)

Scope: `docs/web-roadmap.md` section 6. Scenarios S60, S63, S66. Contract: `listBudgets`, `createBudget`, `getBudget`, `addBudgetAmount`, `getBudgetProgress`, `getCommitted`, `getCategoryTotals` of `api/openapi.yaml`; no contract change.

Region-dependent values: none introduced. Currency comes from the budget's `Money` and from `settings.base_currency`; months are written through `Intl` with the active locale.

## Slice 1: `feat(web): add the budget month view`

- `features/planning/planning-search.ts`: tab `budgets`.
- `features/planning/planning-page.tsx`: tab `budgets`; the header's month control serves the agenda and the budgets.
- `features/planning/budget-month.ts`: pure grouping of budget rows by top-level category, subtotals over the budgets with no budgeted ancestor, unbudgeted expense categories (own total non-zero, no budget on the category or an ancestor).
- `features/planning/budgets-tab.tsx`: committed figures (`getCommitted`, prices in the occurrences note), one table of budgets grouped by top-level category with planned, carried, spent, available and a meter of spent against planned plus carried, a table of unbudgeted spending (`getCategoryTotals`). A budget whose first month follows the chosen month is left out: `getBudgetProgress` answers 422 for it.
- `navigation/navigation-map.json`: budget tasks on `/planning` with topic `budgets`; `internal/assistant/internal/guide/guide_test.go` drops the `budgets` exemption.
- Tests: `features/planning/budgets.test.tsx` (S60 available, overspent, subtotals, unbudgeted list, S66 price), `budget-month.test.ts`.

## Slice 2: `feat(web): add the budget form and detail`

- Routes `/planning/budgets/new` and `/planning/budgets/$id`.
- `features/planning/budget-form-page.tsx`: category, monthly amount, first month, rollover; `409 budget_exists` navigates to the existing budget.
- `features/planning/budget-page.tsx`: amounts by month, add an amount from a month after the latest, chart of planned against spent over the last six months (`getBudgetProgress` per month), link to `/transactions` filtered by category and month.
- `docs/web-roadmap.md`: item 6 delivered.
- Tests: `features/planning/budget-form.test.tsx`, `features/planning/budget-page.test.tsx`.
