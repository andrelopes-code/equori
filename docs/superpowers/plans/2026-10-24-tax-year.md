# Tax year report of investments

Roadmap item 11 (`docs/web-roadmap.md`). Operations `listRealizedResults` and `listIncomeEvents`; scenarios S64 and S65.

The contract takes a calendar `year` (1900 to 2200). The screen reads 1 January to 31 December and never says "fiscal year".

Region-dependent values and their extension point: tax class and tax treatment codes come from the API and are labelled by catalog keys `taxYear.class.<code>` and `taxYear.treatment.<code>` (a code without a key reads as the code); the jurisdiction (`internal/portfolio`, `defaultJurisdiction`) assigns the codes. Income type labels reuse `income.type.<code>`. Numbers, currencies, months and dates go through `Intl` with `tagOf`.

## Slices

1. `feat(web): add the realized results of a year to the portfolio`: route `/portfolio/year`, year selector, tabs, realized results table with totals by month and by tax class, one total per currency.
2. `feat(web): add the income events of a year to the portfolio`: income events table with totals by tax treatment and by income type; roadmap item 11 delivered.
