## Cash view rule

| ID | Rule |
|---|---|
| CV1 | A card line is a posting with card role `charge` or `credit` on a credit card account whose instrument is the base currency. A transaction without a card line counts each category posting on its booked date, as in the accrual view. |
| CV2 | The categories of a card line: in a transaction linked `anticipation_of` or `cancellation_of` to a purchase, each credit takes the purchase's category postings in proportion to the purchase's card total, negated. Every other card line of a transaction takes, in proportion to its amount, the transaction's category postings minus what the credits of the first kind took. A tax on a purchase abroad is a category posting of the purchase, so it enters through its category. |
| CV3 | The composition of a statement is the sum of its card lines' categories. Credits (refunds, reversals) enter it negative. An installment enters the statement it is billed on; an anticipation moves its installments by moving their lines. |
| CV4 | Payments (card role `payment`) are applied in date order, then posting id, to the statements of the card in reference order, oldest first. A statement whose composition is zero or negative merges into the next one. A payment beyond every billed statement is held and applied to later statements, dated on the payment's date. |
| CV5 | A payment of x on a statement whose remaining composition sums to D gives each category c the amount ⌊x·c/D⌋; the cents left go to the category with the largest share, ties to the lowest category id. The category keeps the payment's date. |
| CV6 | A card in another instrument than the base currency follows CV1's second sentence. |

Where it lives: a query computed on read, not a derived table. The share of a payment depends on every earlier line and payment of the card, so a derived row would change on any card write before it, and the one event handler would rebuild a card's whole history per write. One user's card history is read in one query per request. There is no new table, so no migration, no rebuild and no schema invariant.

## Contract

- `getCategoryTotals` gains `view`, `accrual` (default) or `cash`. The name `basis` already means `booked` or `settled` on balance reads, so the new parameter is `view`.
- `getStatementAnalytics` `GET /statements/{id}/analytics`: the statement's totals and one row per category, each with `purchases`, `installments`, `charges`, `credits` and `total`.
- `getCardStatementAnalytics` `GET /accounts/{id}/statement-analytics`: the 12 latest statements of the card whose closing date is on or before today, oldest first, each with the same five totals.

## Statement analytics

Each category amount of a statement's composition (CV3) falls in one part:

| Part | Amount |
|---|---|
| `credits` | every amount from a credit line |
| `charges` | an amount on the system category `interest`, `taxes` or `fees`, or a descendant |
| `installments` | an amount from a line of an installment plan whose purchase precedes the cycle (dated on or before the previous statement's closing), or from a line of a transaction linked `anticipation_of` |
| `purchases` | every other amount |

`total` is the sum of the four parts; on a statement it equals the composition's sum.
