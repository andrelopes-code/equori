# Trades and investment income entry (web roadmap item 9)

Scenarios: S21, S22, S64, S65. Contract: `recordTrade`, `recordInvestmentIncome`, `listInvestmentIncomeTypes`, `listPositions`, `listPrices`.

## Slice 1: `feat(web): record a trade from the entry flow and the security account`

- `trade-math.ts`: exact gross, sale cost (half to even) and cash effect on BigInt; no float.
- `entry-kind.ts`: `PostingKind` for drafts, `EntryKind` adds `trade` and `investment_income`; picker shows the two tiles when the page offers them.
- `trade-form.tsx`: side, security account, cash account of the same brokerage (same parent, base currency), date, quantity, unit price (suggested from `listPrices`, editable), fees, taxes with category, description; live gross and cash effect; on a sell, position and average cost at the date and the estimated result.
- `new-transaction-page.tsx`: the kind chooses the form; success opens the created transaction.
- Entry from the security account page: `/transactions/new?kind=trade&account_id=`.

## Slice 2: `feat(web): record investment income from the entry flow and the security account`

- `income-form.tsx`: security account, cash account, type from `listInvestmentIncomeTypes`, date, gross, withheld with category, category, description; live net.
- `docs/web-roadmap.md`: item 9 Delivered.

## Scope adjustments

- `TradeInput` has no instrument field: the security account holds one instrument, its currency. The form shows it and takes no instrument field.
- The contract has no per-instrument position query: the position comes from `listPositions(date)` filtered by account.

## Regions

Income types and tax treatment come from `listInvestmentIncomeTypes` (ADR 016); no literal type.
