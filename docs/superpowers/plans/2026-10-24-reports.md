# Reports (web roadmap item 12)

Route `/reports`, three tabs routed by the search param `tab`: `net-worth` (default), `categories`, `flows`. Source files live in `web/src/features/reports/`. Words use the `report.` key prefix and live in `web/src/i18n/catalog-reports.{en,pt-BR}.ts`, spread into the two main catalogs.

## Contract

- `getNetWorth`: one call per month end; the current month uses today.
- `getCategoryTotals`: period of at most 366 days, class `income` or `expense`.
- `getAccountFlows`: one account, period of at most 366 days, `FlowDay[]`.
- Out of scope: accrual and cash views and statement analytics (S30), phase P9; no operation is declared.

## Region-dependent values

| Value                                  | Extension point                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Base currency and valuation currencies | `GET /settings`, `GET /instruments`                                                       |
| Amounts, percentages, months, dates    | `Intl` with `tagOf` through `useMoneyFormat`, `formatBasisPoints`, `formatReferenceMonth` |
| Category and account names             | user data                                                                                 |
| Periods                                | civil Gregorian calendar; month, quarter starting 1 January, year                         |

## Slices

1. `feat(web): add the reports destination with net worth over time`: navigation entry, navigation map, route, tabs, net worth series, chart, table, valuation currency, Overview link.
2. `feat(web): add income and expense by category to the reports`: period selector, class, category tree, drill-down, `/transactions` links, period comparison.
3. `feat(web): add flows by account to the reports`: account selector, day or month granularity, comparison, roadmap update.

## Decisions

- The period of a comparison is the neighbouring month, quarter or year, and for a custom period the run of days of the same length that ends the day before it starts.
- The period control is the reports' own (`report-period-control.tsx`): `ui/period-control` has no quarter and steps a quarter as a run of days.
- Percent change is `amount / |base|` in basis points; a zero base shows no percent.
- `price_missing` or any other error of one month removes that month from the chart and lists it under the table.
